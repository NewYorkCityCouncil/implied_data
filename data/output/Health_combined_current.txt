**Section 1, Mission Summary:**

The Department of Health and Mental Hygiene (DOHMH) is responsible for protecting and promoting the health and mental well-being of all city residents, ensuring a safe and healthy environment, and enforcing public health laws. Key programs include vital records registration, medical examiner investigations, regulation of healthcare and mental hygiene services, environmental health oversight, and various public health initiatives for disease prevention and health promotion. The agency impacts constituents by safeguarding their health, ensuring access to essential services, and maintaining a compliant and safe urban environment.
