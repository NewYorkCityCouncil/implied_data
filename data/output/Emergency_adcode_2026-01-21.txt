**Non-Tabular Information:**

*   **Rules and Regulations:** The administrative code itself, and any internal rules or regulations developed by the office.
*   **Job Applications:** Information related to hiring processes.
*   **Receipts:** Financial transaction records, typically stored in accounting systems.
*   **Photos:** Visual documentation of events, damage, or facilities.
*   **Research Documents:** Studies, analyses, or reports that are not raw, tabular data.
*   **Plans (as documents):** The full text of the Food and Water Access Plan, Sheltering Plan, Community Recovery Plan, etc., as distinct documents.
*   **Reports (as documents):** Annual snow preparedness and response reports, emergency management plan assessment reports, cooling center program reports, flash flood outreach reports.
*   **Protocols (as documents):** Weather emergency protocols.
*   **Recommendations (as documents):** Weather emergencies and prolonged utility outage preparedness recommendations for residential and commercial buildings.
*   **Localized Emergency Preparedness Materials (as documents/media):** Flyers, brochures, online content for specific communities.
*   **Public Education Campaign Materials (as documents/media):** Content for heat-related emergency campaigns.
*   **Community Based Organization (CBO) Consultation Records:** Notes or summaries from consultations with CBOs.
*   **Inter-agency Communications:** Records of specific communications between agencies (e.g., emails, meeting minutes).
*   **National Weather Service Consultations:** Records of specific consultations.
*   **Legal Changes:** Updates to laws or regulations affecting the agency.
