**Section 1, Mission Summary:**

The Department of Citywide Administrative Services (DCAS) is responsible for the centralized administration of city government, managing personnel, real estate, procurement, and fleet services. DCAS runs key programs including citywide personnel management, civil service examinations, real property acquisition and disposition, public building maintenance, procurement of goods and services, fleet management, and various employee development and compliance initiatives. The agency's impacts on constituents include ensuring a fair and merit-based city workforce, efficient use of city resources, transparent government operations, and safe public facilities.
