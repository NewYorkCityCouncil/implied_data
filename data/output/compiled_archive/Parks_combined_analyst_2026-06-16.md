    *   **One row represents:** A single park, facility, building, structure, or fixed amenity.

    *   **How it’s implied:** The Charter and Administrative Code mandate DPR to manage, care for, and maintain all parks, public places, playgrounds, beaches, and other recreational properties, including specific features like drinking fountains, statues, buildings, and nurseries. This requires a comprehensive inventory of all assets under its jurisdiction.
        *   (Charter Section 533.a.1, 533.a.3, 533.a.4, 533.a.6) – Mandates management and care for parks, playgrounds, and recreational properties, including construction and maintenance of structures and fixtures.
        *   (Admin Code § 18-104, 18-108, 18-122.b.1, 18-143.a.1, 18-144.a, 18-165.b) – Specifies jurisdiction over public beaches, creation of bicycle areas, reporting on accessible facilities, defining "functional acreage," and establishing compost facilities.
