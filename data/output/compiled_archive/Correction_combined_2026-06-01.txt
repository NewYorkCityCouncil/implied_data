**Section 1, Mission Summary:**

The Department of Correction (DOC) is responsible for the care, custody, and control of all persons incarcerated within the city's correctional institutions, including their transportation, management, and the provision of rehabilitative programs. The agency impacts constituents by ensuring public safety through secure detention, upholding the rights and welfare of incarcerated individuals, and facilitating their rehabilitation and reintegration into society.

**Key Programs:**
*   Management of correctional institutions, hospital wards, and court pens.
*   Care, custody, and transportation of incarcerated individuals.
*   Planning and implementation of re-training, counseling, and rehabilitative programs.
*   Employment of incarcerated individuals in industry, farming, and public works.
*   Investigation of incarcerated individual deaths and grievances.
*   Maintenance and operation of departmental buildings and structures.

*   **Rules and Regulations:** Board of Correction rules, minimum standards for care, custody, correction, treatment, supervision, and discipline.
*   **Reports and Proposals (the documents themselves):** Proposals for capital planning and improvements, studies and reports concerned with correctional program planning, studies and reports in regard to promoting closer cooperation of agencies and courts, annual and other reports, findings, and recommendations from the Board, reports on issues related to the department's grievance process, reports about death investigations, statements posted on the website regarding ongoing death investigations.
*   **Legal Documents/Records:** Written consent from prisoners held for trial to perform work, books, records, documents, and papers of the department, records provided to the board by the department and correctional health services for death investigations, court orders (limiting telephone calls), Directive 6007R-A (for PC adjournments), New York State Correction Law, Prison Rape Elimination Act, New York State Executive Law Article 49-B, Department's policy and procedures (for drug/alcohol testing).
*   **Meeting Minutes:** Records of Board of Correction meetings.
*   **Legal Directives:** Requests or directions from Commissioner of Investigation, Attorney General, or District Attorney not to investigate a death.
*   **Forms/Notices:** Written notice forms (for PHD, security categories, PC, hearings, appeals).
*   **Physical Evidence:** Physical receipts (for inmate property), photos (e.g., of damaged property, contraband), video recordings of hearings.
*   **Inmate Rule Book:** The text itself.
*   **Detailed information relating to incarceration:** Provided separately to inmates.
*   **External Standards:** State Commission of Correction standards (9 NYCRR § 7028.6), New York City Board of Correction standards (40 RCNY § 1-07).
