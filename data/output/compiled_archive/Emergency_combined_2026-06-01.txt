**Section 1, Mission Summary:**

The agency's mission is to coordinate the city's multi-agency response to all emergency conditions and potential incidents, ensuring public safety and health. Key programs include emergency response coordination, preparedness planning, training, public awareness campaigns, resource management, and specific disaster response plans for events like snow, heat, and flash floods. The agency impacts constituents by safeguarding public health and safety, enhancing community preparedness, facilitating rapid response and recovery, and providing critical information during emergencies.
