**Section 1, Mission Summary:**

The Department of Small Business Services (SBS) is dedicated to fostering economic development, supporting businesses, and enhancing workforce opportunities across New York City. They are required to run key programs including business assistance and certification, workforce development, contractor compliance, property management (especially waterfront and airports), and mentoring initiatives. The agency significantly impacts constituents by promoting job creation, ensuring fair business practices, facilitating access to resources, and revitalizing commercial areas.
