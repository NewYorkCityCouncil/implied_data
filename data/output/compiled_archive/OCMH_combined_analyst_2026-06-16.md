# Non-compliant Datasets:

1.  **Mental Health Services Offered**
    *   **Open Data match:** N/A (Open Data list not provided)
    *   **One row represents:** A specific mental health service offered by a provider.
    *   **Covers:** All mental health services provided by relevant agencies and organizations in the city, as reported to the Office.
    *   **How it’s implied:** A required public database means they must have this data. Through § 3-191.a, OCMH is required to maintain a publicly accessible, searchable online database of city mental health services that allow users to search services by target populations, service categories (e.g., crisis, housing, legal, outpatient, substance use) and includes service descriptions, languages offered, delivery method, peer support availability, and disability accessibility information.

2.  **Agencies and Organizations**
    *   **Open Data match:** N/A (Open Data list not provided)
    *   **One row represents:** A single agency or organization.
    *   **Covers:** All city agencies, state agencies, community partners, and other organizations that the OCMH collaborates with or that provide mental health services.
    *   **How it’s implied:** The Charter requires OCMH to coordinate with other Agencies, so they must have this data, at least for the organizations they are working with. Through Charter c.1, d and e.3, OCMH is required to coordinate with “other agencies and offices as needed to support the office's work”. Through Admin. Code § 3-191.a.1, 3, OCMH is required to provide “addresses, office hours, contact information, and accepted payment/insurance information for service providers.”

3.  **Mental Health Emergency Call Responses**
    *   **Open Data match:** N/A (Open Data list not provided)
    *   **One row represents:** A single mental health emergency call and its response.
    *   **Covers:** All 911 calls classified as mental health emergencies, and the subsequent response actions.
    *   **How it’s implied:** Reporting requirements in the Adcode mean they must have this data. Through several reporting requirements (under § 3-196.b) requiring a report on mental health emergency calls every six months, including call volume, dispatch information, B-HEARD involvement, responding entities, response times, assistance outcomes, use-of-force incidents, demographic data and location information.

4.  **Involuntary Mental Health Removals**
    *   **Open Data match:** N/A (Open Data list not provided)
    *   **One row represents:** A single involuntary removal incident.
    *   **Covers:** All involuntary removals conducted under MHL 9.41(a) or 9.58(a) within the city.
    *   **How it’s implied:** Reporting requirements in the Adcode mean they must have this data. Through several reporting requirements (under § 3-194.b) requiring an annual report on involuntary mental health removals conducted during the previous calendar year. Data should include number of involuntary removals by legal authority and the number of 911 calls resulting in an involuntary removal, demographic information (such as age, race, ethnicity, disability status, and homelessness status), information on transportation to hospitals and, where available, hospital admissions.

5.  **Outreach and Dissemination Activities**
    *   **Open Data match:** N/A (Open Data list not provided)
    *   **One row represents:** A single public resource dissemination event or outreach activity.
    *   **Covers:** All public-facing activities aimed at enhancing mental health literacy, promoting access to care, educating about available resources, and culturally appropriate outreach.
    *   **How it’s implied:** The Adcode + Charter require OCMH to conduct outreach, so they are likely tracking the events and methods through which they are conducting outreach. Requirements are based on Admin. Code § 3-192.b (requires OCMH to conduct outreach and educational activities to raise awareness of city mental health resources available to parents), Charter c.4.b (requires disseminating information to improve mental health literacy), Charter e.1.e (requires recommending strategies to educate about mental health and available resources).
