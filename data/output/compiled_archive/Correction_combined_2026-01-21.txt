**Section 1, Mission Summary:**

The agency's mission is to manage the care, custody, and control of all individuals incarcerated in city institutions, ensuring their safety, providing rehabilitative programs, and facilitating their reintegration into the community. Key programs include managing correctional facilities, providing healthcare and mental health services, offering educational and vocational training, overseeing prisoner labor, and implementing discharge planning. The agency's impacts on constituents include ensuring the secure and humane detention of individuals, promoting rehabilitation, and contributing to public safety through effective re-entry.
