**Section 1, Mission Summary:**

The agency is primarily responsible for maintaining the cleanliness of the city, managing waste collection and disposal, and regulating commercial waste operations. They run key programs including street cleaning, waste and recycling collection, snow removal, enforcement of sanitation regulations, and oversight of private waste management facilities and commercial waste zones. The agency's impacts on constituents include ensuring public health and safety through clean streets, efficient waste services, and regulated waste management practices.

**Section 5, Non-tabular Data Summary:**

The agency maintains a significant amount of non-tabular information crucial for its operations and compliance. This includes:

*   **Legal and Regulatory Documents:** The actual text of rules and regulations, public and private hearing transcripts, testimony records, evidence documents, and subpoena documents.
*   **Planning and Design Documents:** Plans, designs, and construction documents for facilities (e.g., architectural drawings, engineering specifications).
*   **Contracts and Agreements:** Full legal agreements for commercial waste removal, insurance policies, and other service contracts.
*   **Business Records:** Internal logs, financial statements, and other records maintained by commercial waste businesses that the department inspects.
*   **Outreach Materials:** Brochures, presentations, videos, and other content used for public and commercial education programs.
*   **Internal Administrative Documents:** Job applications, HR notes, receipts, written notices (e.g., non-collection, past due, suspension/termination), property owner complaint forms, cancellation notices.
*   **Property-Related Documents:** New York City Tax Roll for buildings, certified copies of deeds, leases, subleases, Certificates of Occupancy, written variances for zoning lots.
*   **Financial and Legal Certifications:** Certifications/authorizations from Department of Small Business Services, surety bonds or irrevocable letters of credit, New York State corporation filing receipts, partnership papers, "Doing Business Papers" (for assumed names).
*   **External Permits/Authorizations:** Permits/authorizations from other governmental authorities (e.g., DOB, DOT, DEP, NYSDEC, Army Corps of Engineers).
*   **Technical Specifications/Methodologies:** Written statements from manufacturers (e.g., plastic bag post-consumer material content), Emergency Action Plans, standard smoke charts (Ringelmann chart), U.S. EPA Method 9 and Method 22 (methodologies), manufacturer's rated cubic body capacity for vehicles, approved logos for bags/containers.
*   **Legal/Adjudicatory Records:** Stipulations for multiple claimants of forfeited vehicles, advertisements for public sale of forfeited vehicles, petitions for Rulemaking.
*   **Economic Data:** CPI data (from US Bureau of Labor Statistics).
*   **Visual Records:** Photos (e.g., of overfilled containers, damaged property, vehicle defects).
*   **Community Input:** Community Board resolutions (for early set-out schedules).
