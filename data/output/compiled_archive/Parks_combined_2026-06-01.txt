**Section 1, Mission Summary:**

The Department of Parks and Recreation and New York City Sports Commission is dedicated to the stewardship of the city's public parks, recreational facilities, and natural spaces, while also promoting sports activities. They are required to manage and maintain all park and recreation properties, enforce rules, plan for system improvements, conduct conservation and recreation programs, and oversee the NYC Sports Commission. The agency significantly impacts constituents by providing accessible green spaces, recreational opportunities, and a healthy urban environment.

**Key Programs:**
*   Park and Recreation Property Management and Maintenance
*   Urban Forestry Management
*   Conservation, Environmental, and Nature Education Programs
*   Public Recreation Programs
*   Enforcement of Park Rules and Regulations
*   Capital Project Development and Oversight
*   Management of Marinas and Mooring Fields
*   GreenThumb Community Garden Licensing and Support
*   NYC Sports Commission Governance and Promotion

**Section 5, Non-tabular Data Summary:**

The agency maintains a significant amount of non-tabular information critical to its operations and compliance. This includes formal documents such as plans for park system establishment and improvement, official rules and regulations for park use, and various agreements with partner entities like the Hudson River Park Trust. Research and demonstration project documents, budget estimates from other city agencies for recreation activities, and written notices for Sports Commission representative designations are also kept. Legal changes and provisions of law are implicitly tracked.

From the Administrative Code, non-tabular data includes delegation of authority instruments, written applications for tree care, written approvals from the Commissioner of Transportation, tree site plans, and written notices to permit applicants. Annual reports on gifts, funding, accidents, inspections, and various operational metrics are generated. Lease agreements for numerous facilities, plans and specifications for bicycle/tricycle areas, and gubernatorial certifications/consents are also maintained. Website content, criteria for tree maintenance prioritization, stormwater retention planting manuals, native biodiversity design manuals, and tree removal protocols are key non-tabular assets. Notifications of capital project change orders and diaper changing station exemptions are also documented.

From the Rules, non-tabular information includes job applications, physical receipts, photos (e.g., of gardens, damaged property, park signs), research documents, concession agreements, general license agreements, bylaws, league rosters and schedules, government-issued photo identification, certificates of insurance, vessel registration/documentation, USCG boating safety course completion certificates, marine surveyor evaluations, refund request forms, IDNYC cards, various disability/veteran identification cards, commercial plans, USCG Captain's licenses, bills of sale, written objections/appeals, Garden Review Statements, Retention Agreements, Alternate Site Lists/Notices, Default Notices, Notices of Transfer, written acknowledgments, maps (e.g., expressive matter vending spots, quiet zones), published fee schedules, penalty schedules, and the content of park signs.
