**Section 1, Mission Summary:**

The agency's mission is to guide the physical development of the city through comprehensive planning, zoning, and land use review procedures, ensuring equitable growth and public engagement. They are required to run key programs including the Uniform Land Use Review Procedure (ULURP), environmental reviews, city map maintenance, zoning regulation, city facility planning, and oversight of privately owned public spaces. The agency significantly impacts constituents by shaping the built environment, influencing housing affordability, and determining the location and accessibility of public and private facilities and spaces.
