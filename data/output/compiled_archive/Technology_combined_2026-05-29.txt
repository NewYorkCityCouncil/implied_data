**Section 1, Mission Summary:**

The agency's mission is to manage and enhance New York City's technology and telecommunications infrastructure, services, and data, ensuring efficient operations, public access to information, and robust privacy and security for all city agencies and constituents. They are required to run programs encompassing telecommunications franchise administration, citywide IT and telecom infrastructure provision and maintenance, 311 citizen service management, public data transparency initiatives, and comprehensive data privacy and security enforcement. The agency impacts constituents by providing essential public services, ensuring access to city information, protecting private data, and facilitating a connected and responsive urban environment.

(§ 1-02(b))

    *   In any other location, all conduit must have excess capacity of at least six ducts. (§ 1-03(c))
