**1. Student Academic & Demographic Records**

    *   "the total number of students who have an IEP as of June 30 of the reported academic period, disaggregated by district, eligibility for the free and reduced price lunch program, race/ethnicity, gender, English Language Learner status, status as a student in temporary housing, status as a student in foster care, recommended language of instruction, grade level, disability classification and school" (§ 21-955.b.8)
    *   "For each community school district, school within such district, special program within such school, and grade within such school, the total number of students enrolled in the preceding school year in grades kindergarten through 8 and the number and percentage of such students who: 1. Receive special education services; 2. Are English language learners; 3. Are eligible for the federal free or reduced price meals program; 4. Reside in temporary housing; 5. Are in foster care; 6. Are attending school out of the attendance zone in which the student resides; and 7. Are attending school out of the community school district in which the student resides." (§ 21-957.a)
    *   "The data provided pursuant to subdivision a shall be disaggregated by: 1. Grade level; 2. Race or ethnicity; 3. Gender; 4. Special education status; 5. English language learner status; and 6. Primary home language." (§ 21-957.b)
    *   "For students in grades 3 through 8, the data provided pursuant to subdivision a of this section shall indicate: 1. The number of students who completed the New York state mathematics examination, disaggregated by performance level; and 2. The number of students who completed the New York state English language arts examination, disaggregated by performance level." (§ 21-957.e)
    *   "For each public high school, the total number of students enrolled in grades nine through twelve in the preceding school year and the number and percentage of such students who: 1. Receive special education services; 2. Are English language learners; 3. Are eligible for the federal free or reduced price meals program; 4. Reside in temporary housing; 5. Are in foster care; and 6. Are enrolled over the counter." (§ 21-957.1.a)

*   **Why:** This data is essential for tracking student populations, academic progress, and fulfilling numerous disaggregated reporting requirements.
*   **One row represents:** A student's enrollment record for a specific academic period.
*   **Covers:** All students enrolled in district or charter schools, including pre-kindergarten, across all grades, with their demographic and academic performance details.
*   **Columns:**
    *   **Student ID**
    *   **School ID** (links to Dataset 9)
    *   Academic Period (e.g., 2023-2024)
    *   Grade Level
    *   Race
    *   Ethnicity
    *   Gender
    *   English Language Learner (ELL) Status
    *   Special Education Status (links to Dataset 2, 3, 4, 5)

    *   Status as a Student in Temporary Housing (links to Dataset 6)
    *   Status as a Student in Foster Care
    *   Primary Home Language
    *   Year of Birth
    *   Recommended Language of Instruction
    *   Enrollment Type (e.g., In-zone, Out-of-zone, Out-of-district, Over-the-counter)
    *   State Examination Name (e.g., NYS Math, NYS ELA)
    *   State Examination Score
    *   State Examination Performance Level

    *   3-Year Reevaluation Timeliness (Timely, Not Timely)
    *   Consent to Defer IEP Implementation (Yes/No, if applicable)
    *   Reason for No Consent (for initial evaluation referral, if applicable)

    *   "the total number of students who have an IEP as of June 30 of the reported academic period, disaggregated by... disability classification and school" (§ 21-955.b.8)
    *   "the total number of students within each disability classification referenced in paragraph 8 of this subdivision as of June 30 of the reported academic period" (§ 21-955.b.9)

    *   "the average number of school days between the date the department receives consent... for the initial provision of special education services... and the date the department issues notice of the school that will implement the IEP" (§ 21-955.b.11)
    *   "the number of reevaluations that resulted in an IEP recommendation of more periods per week in a special class... fewer periods per week... removal from a school... placement in a school that serves students who are not students with disabilities" (§ 21-955.b.12)
    *   "the number and percentage of students... who have a behavioral intervention plan" (§ 21-955.b.16)
    *   "the number and percentage of students with IEPs who are recommended for participation in the general education curriculum for: (a) 80% or more of the day; (b) 40-79% of the day; and (c) less than 40% of the day." (§ 21-955.b.17)
    *   "The total number of preschool students with a disability who have an IEP as of June 30 of the reported academic period" (§ 21-955.f.6)
    *   "The number and percentage of preschool students with a disability who, by the end of the academic period, have an IEP that recommends the following enumerated services: Related services only; Monolingual special education itinerant services; Bilingual special education itinerant services; Monolingual full-day integrated special class program; Monolingual half-day integrated special class program; Bilingual full-day integrated special class program; Bilingual half-day integrated special class program; Monolingual full-day special class... Monolingual half-day special class... Bilingual full-day special class... Bilingual half-day special class..." (§ 21-955.f.8)
    *   "The number and percentage of students who have an individualized education program that recommends adaptive physical education, disaggregated by classification of disability" (§ 21-960.b.4)
*   **Why:** This dataset captures the detailed recommendations and classifications from each student's Individualized Education Program.
*   **One row represents:** A specific Individualized Education Program (IEP) for a student.
*   **Covers:** All IEPs developed for students with disabilities, including preschool students, detailing their disability classification, recommended services, and educational placement.
*   **Columns:**
    *   IEP ID
    *   **Student ID** (links to Dataset 1)
    *   Date IEP Developed/Revised
    *   Disability Classification
    *   Most Restrictive Service Recommendation (e.g., Related Services Only, Special Class in D75, Non-Public School Placement)
    *   Recommended Related Services (e.g., Monolingual Speech Therapy, Occupational Therapy, Special Transportation)
    *   Recommended Special Education Program (e.g., Integrated Co-Teaching, Special Education Itinerant Services)
    *   Special Class Ratio (for preschool, e.g., 6:1:2, 8:1:2)
    *   Behavioral Intervention Plan (BIP) (Yes/No)
    *   General Education Curriculum Participation Percentage (e.g., 80%+, 40-79%, <40%)
    *   Adaptive Physical Education Recommended (Yes/No)
    *   Date Notice of School Implementation Issued
    *   Previous IEP ID (for reevaluations, links to itself)
    *   Change in Special Class Periods (More, Fewer, No Change, N/A)
    *   Change in School Placement (Removed from general ed school, Placed in general ed school, No Change, N/A)

*   **Why:** This dataset tracks the actual provision of special education services against IEP recommendations, crucial for compliance and student support.
*   **One row represents:** A specific special education service recommended in an IEP for a student during a reporting period.
*   **Covers:** The status of all recommended special education services (including related services, special education programs, and adaptive physical education) for students with IEPs, indicating full compliance, partial compliance, or awaiting services.
*   **Columns:**
    *   Service Provision ID
    *   **IEP ID** (links to Dataset 3)
    *   **Student ID** (links to Dataset 1)
    *   Service Type (e.g., Monolingual Speech Therapy, Integrated Co-Teaching, Adaptive PE)
    *   Service Status (Full Compliance, Partial Compliance, Awaiting Provision)
    *   Reporting Period End Date
    *   Date Service Arranged (for preschool initial provision)
    *   Arrangement Timeliness (within 60 school days, > 60 school days)

*   **Covers:** All required distributions of informational materials, including those on gifted and talented programs, summer meals, legal rights, FERPA, immigration, bullying portals, civil service exams, bus ridership, suicide prevention, IDNYC, afterschool programs, police youth programs, interpretation/translation services, journalism programs, water safety, drugs/opiates, wellness clubs, mental health literacy, and menstrual product education.
*   **Columns:**
    *   Distribution ID
    *   Academic Year
    *   Material Type (e.g., Gifted & Talented Info, Summer Meals Info, Legal Rights, FERPA Opt-Out, Civil Service Exam Info, Bus Ridership Guide, Suicide Prevention, IDNYC, Afterschool Programs, Police Youth Programs, Interpretation/Translation Services, Journalism Programs, Water Safety, Drugs/Opiates, Wellness Club Toolkit, Mental Health Literacy Training, Menstrual Product Education)
    *   **School ID** (links to Dataset 9, if distributed to school)
    *   **Student ID** (links to Dataset 1, if distributed to student)
    *   Recipient Type (e.g., School, Student, Parent, Employee, Afterschool Program Director, Elected Official)
    *   Date of Distribution
    *   Method of Distribution (e.g., Hard Copy, Electronic Mail, Website Post, Online Portal)
    *   Distributed Upon Request (Yes/No, if applicable)
    *   Number of Bathrooms/Areas (if Menstrual Products)
    *   Dispensing Method (if Menstrual Products)
