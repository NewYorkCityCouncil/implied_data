**Section 1, Mission Summary:**

The Department of Parks and Recreation's core mission is to manage, maintain, and improve public parks, recreational facilities, and natural areas throughout the city, providing diverse opportunities for recreation, conservation, and cultural enrichment. The agency ensures safe, accessible, and beautiful public spaces while regulating activities within its jurisdiction.

**Key Programs:**
*   Park and Green Space Management (including tree care, conservation, and beautification)
*   Recreation Facility and Program Operation (including sports, swimming, and educational activities)
*   Permitting and Enforcement of Park Rules
*   Management of Marine Facilities (marinas and mooring fields)
*   Capital Project Development and Accessibility Improvement
*   Partnership and Concession Management

**Impacts the Agency has on constituents:**
The agency provides essential public spaces and programs that enhance the health, welfare, and quality of life for all city residents, fostering community engagement and protecting natural resources.
