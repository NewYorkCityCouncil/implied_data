**Section 1, Mission Summary:**

The Department of Parks and Recreation is responsible for the comprehensive management, care, and improvement of all public parks, recreational facilities, and related properties within the city, while also planning and supervising diverse recreation and environmental programs for the public. The agency impacts constituents by providing accessible, safe, and well-maintained green spaces, recreational opportunities, and cultural facilities, enhancing public health, welfare, and community engagement.

**Key Programs:**
*   Management and care of parks, playgrounds, public places, and recreational properties.
*   Planning and execution of a city-wide park system and beautification initiatives.
*   Establishment and enforcement of rules and regulations for park use and protection.
*   Planning, conducting, and supervising recreation, conservation, and environmental education programs.
*   Operation and maintenance of specialized facilities such as zoos, botanical gardens, marinas, and athletic complexes.
*   Issuance and management of various permits for park use, events, and commercial activities.
*   Inspection, maintenance, and capital improvement of park infrastructure, including trees, facilities, and public amenities.
*   Management of community gardens through the GreenThumb program.
*   Ensuring accessibility and safety across all park facilities and programs.

*   "Urban Park Rangers provide programs on environmental education, outdoor recreation, wildlife management, and active conservation, including: (1) Weekend Adventure: offered to the public year-round at no cost to participants; (2) Natural Classroom: offered to schools, youth groups and other organizations for a fee; (3) Custom Adventures: day and overnight programs offered to camps and other youth groups for a fee; and (4) Adventure Course: offered to groups of all ages on the low- and high-ropes course at Alley Pond Park for a fee" - Rules 2-15.a
*   "The Urban Park Rangers may cancel a program for the following reasons: (1) Inclement or severe weather... (2) A group that has not paid the entire fee for a scheduled program may not attend the program." - Rules 2-15.b
*   "A group that has scheduled and fully paid for a Natural Classroom, Custom Adventure, or Adventure Course Program and then cancels the program is subject to the following policies" - Rules 2-15.c
*   "The Commissioner shall charge the amounts set forth in the below schedule for Urban Park Ranger programs." - Rules 2-15.d

**Why:** To manage registrations, track participation, and handle cancellations and refunds for Urban Park Ranger programs.

**One row represents:** A single registration for an Urban Park Ranger program.

**Columns:**
*   **UPR Program Registration ID** (Unique identifier for each registration)
*   Program Name (e.g., "Weekend Adventure," "Natural Classroom," "Custom Adventure - Day," "Adventure Course")
*   Program Type (e.g., "Environmental Education," "Outdoor Recreation," "Wildlife Management")
*   **Facility ID** (Links to Dataset 2: Facility Inventory, if applicable, e.g., Alley Pond Park)
*   Registration Date
*   Participant/Group Name
*   Participant/Group Type (e.g., "Individual," "School," "Youth Group," "Camp," "Adult/Staff Development Group")
*   Number of Participants (Rules 2-15.d)
*   Number of Chaperones (for overnight programs) (Rules 2-15.d)
*   Fee Charged
*   Fee Payment Status
*   Program Date
*   Program Time
*   Program Status (e.g., "Scheduled," "Completed," "Cancelled by UPR," "Cancelled by Group")
*   Reason for Cancellation (if applicable) (Rules 2-15.b,c)
*   Refund/Credit Issued (Yes/No) (Rules 2-15.b.1,c)
*   Refund Amount/Credit Value
*   Credit Expiration Date (if applicable) (Rules 2-15.b.1,c)
*   Title I Funding Eligibility (for schools) (Rules 2-15.d)
*   Lunch Provided (for Adventure Course) (Rules 2-15.d)

**31. School Playground Public Access Report Data**

**Quotes:**
*   "No later than 75 days after the effective date of the local law that added this section, and annually thereafter, the department of education, in consultation with the department and the school construction authority, shall submit to the mayor and the speaker of the council a report about playgrounds to which the department of education may provide access to the public after school on weekdays when school is in session, and from 8:00 a.m. to dusk on weekends and on weekdays when school is not in session." - Admin Code 18-167.b
*   "Such report shall prioritize access to playgrounds located in environmental justice areas." - Admin Code 18-167.b
*   "Such report shall include, but need not be limited to: 1. A list of no less than 15 playgrounds, including no less than 1 playground located in an environmental justice area, that the department of education may operate and maintain for use by the public... and the reason the department of education included each such playground on such list; 2. A description of the duties and responsibilities of the department and the department of education in relation to operating and maintaining any playground on such list; 3. The estimated budget required to operate and maintain all such playgrounds...; and 4. Any anticipated challenges in relation to providing access to the public to any such playground during such days and times." - Admin Code 18-167.b.1-4

**Why:** To track school playgrounds identified for public access, including their location, operational details, budget, and challenges, for inter-agency reporting and planning.

**One row represents:** A single school playground identified in the public access report.

**Columns:**
*   **School Playground ID** (Unique identifier for each playground)
*   Report Year
*   School Name
*   Playground Address/Location
*   Borough
*   Community District
*   Located in Environmental Justice Area (Yes/No) (18-167.b)
*   Included in Report (Yes/No)
*   Reason for Inclusion in Report (18-167.b.1)
*   Public Access Provided (Yes/No)
*   Public Access Hours (e.g., "After school weekdays," "Weekends 8am-dusk")
*   DPR Duties/Responsibilities (Description) (18-167.b.2)
*   DOE Duties/Responsibilities (Description) (18-167.b.2)
*   Estimated Annual Operating Budget (18-167.b.3)
*   Anticipated Challenges (Description) (18-167.b.4)
*   Existing Public Access Prior to Report (Yes/No) (18-167.b)
