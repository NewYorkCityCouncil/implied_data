**Section 1, Mission Summary:**
The agency is responsible for coordinating New York City's comprehensive emergency management efforts, including planning, response, recovery, and public awareness, to protect public health and safety from a wide range of hazards. Key programs include multi-agency emergency response coordination, citywide emergency plan development and review, public awareness campaigns, and specialized recovery efforts for vulnerable populations and businesses. The agency's impacts on constituents include enhanced safety, rapid and coordinated emergency services, and improved resilience and recovery capabilities across the city.

*   **Photos:** Visual documentation of events, damage, or facilities.
*   **Research documents:** Studies, analyses, or reports.

*   **Software contracts:** The contracts themselves, not their status or details.
*   **Environmental compliance documents:** The documents themselves.
*   **Building repair specifications:** Detailed specifications for repairs.
*   **Uniform provision specifications:** Detailed specifications for uniforms.
*   **Detailed content of public awareness materials:** The actual content of materials.
*   **Detailed content of training programs:** The actual content of training programs.
*   **Community Based Organization (CBO) Consultation Records:** Notes or summaries from consultations.
