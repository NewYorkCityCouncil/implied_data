**Section 1, Mission Summary:**

This agency is responsible for ensuring transparent, compliant, and efficient procurement and financial management across all city operations, with a strong emphasis on social equity, labor standards, and economic development. Key programs include contract oversight, M/WBE/EBE utilization, living and prevailing wage enforcement, predatory lending prevention, and disaster recovery project management. The agency's actions directly impact the fairness of contracting opportunities for businesses, the economic well-being of workers, and the responsible use of public funds for constituents.
