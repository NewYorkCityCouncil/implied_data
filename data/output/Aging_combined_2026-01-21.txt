**Section 1, Mission Summary:**

The Department for the Aging (NYC Aging) is mandated to promote the well-being of older adults in New York City by coordinating and funding programs, operating facilities, and advocating for their needs. The agency is required to run key programs including financial assistance and grants, facility and program operations, advisory and cabinet councils, community engagement, referrals, local program development, housing support, caregiver support, aging in place initiatives, and digital literacy training. NYC Aging significantly impacts constituents by providing essential services, ensuring facility safety, addressing complaints, and advocating for policies that enhance the quality of life for older adults and their caregivers.
