Section 1, Mission Summary:
The agency's mission is to coordinate, manage, and monitor New York City's preparedness and response to all types of emergencies and potential incidents requiring a multi-agency effort. It oversees citywide emergency planning, public awareness, training, and resource coordination to protect public health and safety. The agency directly impacts constituents by ensuring a coordinated and effective emergency response, enhancing public safety, and providing essential services during crises.

Section 3, Data Details:

1.  **Emergency Incidents & Response Log**
    *   **Quotes:**
        *   "coordinate the city's response to all emergency conditions and potential incidents which require a multi-agency response, including but not limited to severe weather, threats from natural hazards and natural disasters, power and other public service outages, labor unrest other than the keeping of the peace, water main breaks, transportation and transit incidents, hazardous substance discharges, building collapses, aviation disasters, explosions, acts of terrorism and such other emergency conditions and incidents which affect public health and safety;" (Section 497.a)
        *   "monitor on a constant basis all potential emergency conditions and potential incidents which may require a multi-agency response;" (Section 497.b)
        *   "operate an emergency operations center to assist the city in managing emergency conditions and potential incidents that may require a multi-agency response;" (Section 497.g)
        *   "coordinate New York city's civil defense effort in accordance with the provisions of the Defense Emergency Act of New York state and the city's civil defense emergency operations plan, as such plan may be amended from time to time;" (Section 497.m)
    *   **Why:** Tracks all emergencies and potential threats, central to coordinating response and EOC operations.
    *   **One row represents:** A single emergency condition, potential incident, or civil defense event.
    *   **Important columns:** Incident ID, Incident Type, Incident Status, Start Date/Time, End Date/Time, Location (address, coordinates), Impacted Area, Agencies Notified, EOC Activation Status, Resources Deployed (links to 3), Commissioner Recommendations, Public Information Released (links to 7), Incident Description, Severity Level, Multi-Agency Response Required.

2.  **Resource Deployment Log**
    *   **Quotes:**
        *   "All agencies further shall promptly provide the department with all appropriate material, equipment and resources needed for emergency management functions, including personnel." (Section 498.a)
        *   "coordinate the city's response to all emergency conditions and potential incidents which require a multi-agency response" (Section 497.a)
        *   "operate an emergency operations center to assist the city in managing emergency conditions and potential incidents that may require a multi-agency response;" (Section 497.g)
    *   **Why:** Records the allocation and deployment of resources during incidents for effective management.
    *   **One row represents:** A single deployment event of a resource to an incident.
    *   **Important columns:** Deployment ID, Incident ID (links to 1), Resource ID (links to 4 or 11), Resource Type, Quantity Deployed, Deployment Start Date/Time, Deployment End Date/Time, Destination/Purpose, Status, Agency Providing Resource.

3.  **Emergency Plans Register**
    *   **Quotes:**
        *   "prepare plans for responding to emergency conditions and potential incidents, including but not limited to plans for the implementation of such emergency orders as may be approved by the mayor to protect public safety and facilitate the rapid response and mobilization of agencies and resources;" (Section 497.d)
        *   "coordinate with all city agencies to ensure that all such agencies develop, implement and periodically review citywide emergency plans;" (Section 497.j)
        *   "No later than two years after the effective date of the local law that created this subdivision, and every two years thereafter, the department shall convene relevant agencies to review citywide emergency plans coordinated by the department." (Section 498.b)
        *   "Following each review conducted pursuant to subdivision b of this section, the department shall publish on its website a list of citywide emergency plans coordinated by the department and a summary of each such plan, including but not limited to information on the roles and responsibilities of relevant agencies and other partners." (Section 498.c)
    *   **Why:** Manages the development, review, and publication of all citywide emergency plans.
    *   **One row represents:** A single citywide emergency plan.
    *   **Important columns:** Plan ID, Plan Title, Plan Version, Creation Date, Last Review Date, Next Review Due Date, Lead Agency, Contributing Agencies (links to 6), Summary (for public disclosure), Publication Status, Public Comment Period Start Date, Public Comment Period End Date, Mayor's Determination for Non-Publication (if applicable).

4.  **Inter-Agency Resource Inventory**
    *   **Quotes:**
        *   "All agencies further shall promptly provide the department with all appropriate material, equipment and resources needed for emergency management functions, including personnel." (Section 498.a)
    *   **Why:** Tracks available external resources from other agencies for emergency management functions.
    *   **One row represents:** A specific resource (piece of equipment, type of material, personnel skill) offered by an external agency.
    *   **Important columns:** Resource ID, Agency ID (links to 6), Agency Name, Resource Type, Resource Name/Description, Quantity Available, Location, Contact Person, Availability Status, Last Updated Date.

5.  **Training and Drill Events**

    *   **Important columns:** Training/Drill ID, Event Type, Title, Description, Date, Time, Location, Instructor/Facilitator, Target Audience, Agencies Participating (links to 6), Number of Participants, Evaluation/Outcome Summary, Associated Incident Type.

        *   "the department shall convene relevant agencies to review citywide emergency plans coordinated by the department." (Section 498.b)
    *   **Why:** Records details of inter-agency meetings for coordination and plan review.

    *   **Important columns:** Meeting ID, Meeting Type, Date, Time, Location, Chair/Host Agency, Participating Agencies, Attendees Count, Agenda, Key Decisions/Action Items, Related Plan ID (links to 3).

7.  **Public Information and Engagement Log**
