**Section 1, Mission Summary:**

The agency's mission is to manage, care for, maintain, and improve all public parks, recreational properties, and related facilities, while also promoting conservation, environmental education, and sports activities for the public. Key programs include park system planning and development, property and asset maintenance, rule enforcement, conservation and recreation program delivery, and sports promotion. The agency impacts constituents by providing accessible, safe, and well-maintained public spaces for recreation, education, and community engagement, and by fostering a healthy environment for sports.
