**Section 1, Mission Summary:**

The Department of Transportation (DOT) is responsible for controlling and operating all city functions related to transportation infrastructure and services. Its mission is to ensure the safe, efficient, and equitable movement of people and goods across the city's streets, highways, bridges, tunnels, and waterways, while also regulating various for-hire vehicle services. The agency impacts constituents by maintaining critical infrastructure, managing traffic flow, enforcing parking and traffic regulations, providing ferry services, and overseeing licensed transportation providers, all of which directly affect daily commutes, public safety, and urban mobility.

**Key Programs:**
*   Parking and Traffic Operations
*   Highway Operations (construction, maintenance, repair of roads, streets, bridges, tunnels)
*   Ferry Operations and Maintenance
*   Mass Transportation Facilities Planning
*   Street and Sidewalk Permitting and Enforcement
*   Traffic Safety Initiatives (e.g., speed limits, traffic calming, crash analysis)
*   Regulation and Licensing of For-Hire Vehicles (Taxicabs, Coaches, Commuter Vans, High-Volume For-Hire Services)
*   School Bus Safety and Operations
*   Greenway and Pedestrian Plaza Development
*   Capital Project Tracking
