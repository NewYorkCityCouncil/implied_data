**Section 1, Mission Summary:**

The agency's mission is to provide social services, public assistance, and care to eligible individuals and families, ensuring their well-being, safety, and access to essential resources. Key programs include social services case management, public assistance (cash, SNAP, Medicaid), child welfare, child care oversight, housing support, elder abuse prevention, and public health initiatives. The agency significantly impacts constituents by providing a safety net, facilitating access to critical benefits, ensuring child safety, and maintaining public health and welfare standards.
