**Section 1, Mission Summary:**

The Department of Probation supervises individuals sentenced to probation, ensuring compliance with court orders and facilitating rehabilitation, while also managing youth intake and adjustment cases. The agency's work directly impacts constituents by guiding individuals through their probation terms, supporting youth development, and ensuring public safety through supervision and program engagement.
