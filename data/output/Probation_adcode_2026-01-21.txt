**Data Systems for the Department of Probation**

**1. Probationer Master Data**
*   **OpenData Match:** Not applicable (no access to OpenData).
*   **Quotes:**
    *   § 9-203.a "Probationer. The term "probationer" means a person the department has been ordered to supervise."
    *   § 9-203.c.1 "The age of the probationer, where applicable, in the following categories at a minimum: (a) up to age 15, (b) 16 - 18, (c) 18 - 24, and (d) 25 and older. For the purposes of subdivision b of this section, such age shall be calculated by using the probationer’s age at the end of the reporting period, and for the purposes of subdivision c of this section such age shall be calculated by using the probationer’s age at the time at which their period of supervision began;"
    *   § 9-203.c.2 "Whether the underlying case for which the probationer was ordered to be monitored by the department was classified by state law, or by equivalent laws of another state, as a: (a) juvenile delinquency, (b) juvenile offender, (c) youthful offender, or (d) adult criminal case;"
    *   § 9-203.c.5 "The risk level of the probationer, as described in section 351.6 of title 9 of the compilation of codes, rules and regulations of the state of New York, or any successor regulation."
*   **Why:** Essential for identifying and categorizing individuals under supervision and for meeting various reporting requirements.
*   **One row represents:** A single individual under the department's supervision.
*   **Covers:** All persons the Department of Probation has been ordered to supervise.
*   **Columns:** Probationer ID, Gender, Race, Date of Birth, Probation Start Date, Expected Probation End Date, Underlying Case Type, Most Serious Offense (if applicable), Initial Risk Level, Current Probation Status.

**2. Probationer Supervision Events**
*   **OpenData Match:** Not applicable (no access to OpenData).
*   **Quotes:**
    *   § 9-203.b.2 "The number and percentage of probationers who were: (a) arrested for a non-criminal offense; (b) arrested for any crime; (c) arrested for a misdemeanor; (d) arrested for a felony; disaggregated by whether such felony is a violent felony offense as such term is defined by section 70.02 of the penal law or any successor statute, or whether such probationers were felony drug offenders or second felony drug offenders, as such terms are defined by section 70.70 of the penal law or any successor statute; (e) convicted of a non-criminal offense, (f) convicted of a misdemeanor; or (g) convicted of a felony, disaggregated by whether such felony is a violent felony offense as such term is defined by section 70.02 of the penal law or any successor statute, or whether such probationers were felony drug offenders or second felony drug offenders, as such terms are defined by section 70.70 of the penal law or any successor statute;"
    *   § 9-203.b.3 "With respect to the probationers who were arrested for any non-criminal offense, misdemeanor or felony during their probation, as reported in paragraph 2 of this subdivision, the number of arrests that resulted in sentences of incarceration other than time served, disaggregated by such category of arrest;"
    *   § 9-203.b.4 "The number and percentage of probationers who were arrested within the following periods of time from the date of their sentence: (a) 1 month, (b) 3 months, (c) 6 months, (d) 1 year, (e) 2 years, and (f) 3 years;"
    *   § 9-203.b.5 "The number and percentage of probationers who: (a) were in full compliance with the terms of their probation, (b) violated the terms of their probation, in total and disaggregated by whether such violation was based on an arrest or another ground, or (c) violated the terms of their probation, and there was filed a related declaration of delinquency, petition of violation, or similar court filing, in total and disaggregated by whether such declaration was based on an arrest, a violation of the technical terms of probation, or absconding;"
    *   § 9-203.b.6 "The number and percentage of probationers whose period of probation was successfully completed during the reporting period, and the mean and median length of their period of probation;"
    *   § 9-203.c.4 "For probationers who committed a technical violation of their probation resulting in a related declaration of delinquency, petition of violation, or similar court filing, the specific probation conditions violated, disaggregated by the age ranges specified in paragraph 1 of this subdivision;"
    *   § 9-206.a "The department of probation shall not require individuals on probation to submit to marijuana testing unless a determination is made, based on an individual's history and personal circumstances, that abstinence from marijuana is necessary to otherwise lead an otherwise law-abiding life."
*   **Why:** Tracks all critical events and compliance status changes for probationers to meet extensive reporting requirements.
*   **One row represents:** A single significant event or status update during a probationer's supervision.
*   **Covers:** All arrests, convictions, violations, compliance checks, marijuana testing decisions, and probation completion statuses for supervised individuals.
*   **Columns:** Event ID, **Probationer ID (linked to Dataset 1)**, Event Date, Event Type (e.g., Arrest, Conviction, Violation, Compliance Check, Marijuana Testing Decision, Probation End, Declaration of Delinquency, Absconding, Successful Completion), Event Details (e.g., Charge, Conviction Type, Violation Description, Specific Condition Violated, Marijuana Testing Required Y/N, Test Result), Arrest Category (e.g., Non-Criminal, Misdemeanor, Felony, Violent Felony, Drug Offender), Conviction Category, Resulted in Incarceration (Y/N), Compliance Status at Event, Age at Event, Risk Level at Event.

**4. Probationer Financial Transactions**
*   **OpenData Match:** Not applicable (no access to OpenData).
*   **Quotes:**
    *   § 9-201.a "any individual currently serving or who shall be sentenced to a period of probation upon conviction of any crime under article thirty-one of the vehicle and traffic law shall pay to the department of probation an administrative fee of thirty dollars per month."
    *   § 9-201.d "The department of probation shall waive all or part of the administrative fee where, because of the indigence of the offender, the payment of the administrative fee would work an unreasonable hardship on the person convicted, his or her immediate family, or any other person who is dependent on such person for financial support."
    *   § 9-201.e "In the event of non-payment of any fees that have not been waived by the department of probation, the city of New York may seek to enforce payment in any manner permitted by law for enforcement of a debt."
    *   § 9-202.a "In accordance with section 252-a of the family court act, when ordered by the court to conduct an investigation pursuant to section six hundred fifty-three of the family court act, the department of probation shall receive an investigation fee of not less than fifty dollars and not more than five hundred dollars from the parties in such proceeding for performing such investigation."
    *   § 9-202.b "Such investigation fee shall be determined by the court based on the party's ability to pay the fee, and the schedule for payment shall be fixed by the court issuing the order for investigation, pursuant to the guidelines issued by the director of the New York state division of probation and correctional alternatives."
    *   § 9-202.c "The court, in its discretion, may waive the investigation fee when the parties lack sufficient means to pay the fee."
    *   § 9-202.d "The court shall apportion the investigation fee between the parties based upon the respective financial circumstances of the parties and the equities of the case."
*   **Why:** Tracks all fees owed, paid, and waived by probationers and other parties to ensure legal compliance and revenue collection.
*   **One row represents:** A single fee transaction (payment, waiver, or billing event).
*   **Covers:** All administrative fees from probationers and investigation fees from parties in family court proceedings.
*   **Columns:** Transaction ID, **Probationer ID (linked to Dataset 1, if applicable)**, Party Name (for investigation fees), Fee Type (Administrative, Investigation), Date Due, Amount Due, Date Paid, Amount Paid, Waiver Status (Full, Partial, None), Waiver Reason, Court Order ID (for investigation fees), Apportionment Details (for investigation fees), Collection Action Taken.

*   **OpenData Match:** Not applicable (no access to OpenData).
*   **Quotes:**
    *   § 9-205.b "The department may only honor a civil immigration detainer by holding a person if: 1. federal immigration authorities present the department with a judicial warrant for the detention of the person who is the subject of such civil immigration detainer at the time such civil immigration detainer is presented; and 2. a search of state and federal databases, or any similar or successor databases, accessed through the New York state division of criminal justice services e-JusticeNY computer application, or any similar or successor computer application maintained by the city or state of New York, indicates, or the department has been informed by a court or any other governmental entity, that such person: (a) has been convicted of a violent or serious crime, or (b) is identified as a possible match in the terrorist screening database."

    *   § 9-205.f.2 "the number of persons held pursuant to civil immigration detainers;"
    *   § 9-205.f.3 "the number of persons transferred to the custody of federal immigration authorities pursuant to civil immigration detainers;"
