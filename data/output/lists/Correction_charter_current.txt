(Section 623.5)
*   "General supervision and responsibility for the planning and implementation of re-training, counseling and rehabilitative programs for felons, misdemeanants and violators of local laws who have been sentenced and are held in institutions under his charge." (Section 623.6)
*   "Every incarcerated individual of an institution under the authority of the commissioner shall be employed in some form of industry, in farming operations or other employment" (Section 625)

*   "Such report shall incorporate direct feedback from incarcerated individuals and proposed recommendations for relevant improvements, and shall include a section of recommendations on how to improve the grievance process for vulnerable populations, including incarcerated individuals who are lesbian, gay, bisexual, transgender, intersex, and gender nonconforming." **Why:** This dataset is fundamental for managing the identity, legal status, and core needs of every individual under DOC custody.

**One row represents:** A single incarcerated individual.

**Covers:** All persons held or confined under the jurisdiction of the Department of Correction.

**Columns:**
*   Individual ID
*   Full Name
*   Date of Birth
*   Gender
*   Legal Status (e.g., Felon, Misdemeanant, Violator of Local Law, Awaiting Arraignment, Awaiting Trial, Sentenced, Witness)
*   Arrest Date
*   Admission Date to DOC Custody
*   Expected Release Date
*   Actual Release Date
*   Sentence Details (if sentenced)
*   Psychiatric Observation/Treatment Required (Y/N)
*   Hospital Care Required (Y/N)
*   Vulnerable Population Flag (e.g., LGBTQI+, based on self-identification or assessment)
*   Next of Kin Name
*   Next of Kin Contact Information
*   Death Date (if applicable)

**3. Incarcerated Individual Death Investigations**

**Quotes:**
*   "Except where the commissioner of investigation, the attorney general, or the district attorney for the county in which a death of an incarcerated individual held or confined under the jurisdiction of the department occurred investigates such death or prosecutes any alleged criminal offense related to such death and requests or directs the board not to investigate such death, the board shall investigate such death and prepare a report about such investigation." (Section 626.i.2)
*   "Such report shall include any recommendations about measures the department or correctional health services may implement to prevent the circumstances that contributed to the individual’s death." (Section 626.i.2)
*   "The board shall submit such report to the department and correctional health services for review by the department and correctional health services pursuant to subdivision h of section 9-171 of the administrative code." (Section 626.i.2.a)
*   "No later than 30 days after the board submits the report to the department and correctional health services, the board shall post such report and any response to such report from the department and correctional health services on the website of the board" (Section 626.i.2.b)
*   "where the board has not concluded an investigation of a death of an incarcerated individual pursuant to this subdivision within 180 days of such death, the board shall, every 60 days until the board concludes such investigation and posts a report about such investigation, post on its website a statement that the board has not concluded such investigation" (Section 626.i.3)
*   "Upon request from the next of kin of an incarcerated individual who has died, the board shall provide to such next of kin records provided to the board by the department and correctional health services for purposes of such investigation" (Section 626.i.4)

**Why:** This dataset tracks mandated investigations into incarcerated individual deaths, ensuring accountability and informing preventative measures.

**One row represents:** A single investigation into the death of an incarcerated individual.

**Covers:** All deaths of incarcerated individuals held or confined under the jurisdiction of the department.

**Columns:**
*   Investigation ID
*   Individual ID (linked to Dataset 1)
*   Date of Death
*   Time of Death
*   Location of Death (Facility ID, linked to Dataset 5)
*   Primary Cause of Death
*   Contributing Factors to Death
*   Investigating Authority (e.g., Board of Correction, Commissioner of Investigation, Attorney General, District Attorney)
*   Investigation Start Date
*   Investigation Conclusion Date
*   Report Submission Date to Department/Correctional Health Services
*   Report Posting Date on Board Website
*   Date of 60-day Status Statement Posting (if applicable)
*   Recommendations Made (summary or link to document)
*   Next of Kin Records Request Date
*   Next of Kin Records Provided Date

**14. Board of Correction Member Roster**

**15. Board of Correction Investigation Cases**

**Quotes:**
*   "The board, or by written designation of the board, any member of it, the executive director, or other employee, shall have the following powers and duties: 1. The inspection and visitation at any time of all institutions and facilities under the jurisdiction of the department; 2. The inspection of all books, records, documents, and papers of the department;" (Section 626.c)
*   "The board, annually and at such other times as it may determine, shall submit to the mayor, the council, and the commissioner reports, findings and recommendations in regard to the matters within its jurisdiction." (Section 626.d)
*   "Within the scope of its authority pursuant to this section, the board may compel the attendance of witnesses, require the production of books, accounts, papers and other evidence, administer oaths, examine persons, and conduct public or private hearings, studies and investigations." (Section 626.g)
*   "The board may institute proceedings in a court of appropriate jurisdiction to enforce its subpoena power and other authority pursuant to this section." (Section 626.g)
*   "The board, or by written designation, a member of the board or the executive director, may conduct hearings, or study or investigate any matter within the jurisdiction of the department, and the board may make recommendations and submit reports of its findings to the appropriate authorities." (Section 626.h)

**Why:** This dataset documents the Board of Correction's exercise of its broad investigative powers, tracking cases and their outcomes.

**One row represents:** A single investigation, study, or hearing initiated by the Board of Correction.

**Covers:** All investigations, studies, and hearings conducted by the Board of Correction or its designees into matters within the department's jurisdiction.

*   End Date

*   Key Findings (summary or link to detailed report)
*   Recommendations Made (summary or link to detailed report)
*   Report Submission Date to Authorities
*   Court Proceedings Initiated (Y/N)
*   Outcome of Court Proceedings (if applicable)

    *   Reports about death investigations (Section 626.i.2)
    *   Statements posted on the website regarding ongoing death investigations (Section 626.i.3)
*   **Legal Documents/Records:**
    *   Written consent from prisoners held for trial to perform work (Section 625)
    *   Books, records, documents, and papers of the department (Section 626.c.2, S626.g)
    *   Records provided to the board by the department and correctional health services for death investigations (Section 626.i.4)
*   **Meeting Minutes:**
    *   Records of Board of Correction meetings (implied by "adopt rules to govern its own proceedings" S626.b)
*   **Legal Directives:**
    *   Requests or directions from Commissioner of Investigation, Attorney General, or District Attorney not to investigate a death (Section 626.i.2, S626.i.3)
