**1. Fire & Emergency Incidents**

**Quotes:**
- "The department shall have sole and exclusive power and authority to extinguish fires at any place within the jurisdiction of the city and shall have power and authority to extinguish fires upon any vessel in the port of New York or upon any dock, wharf, pier, warehouse or other structure bordering upon or adjacent to such port." (Section 487.b)
- "The investigation of the cause, circumstances and origin of fires and the suppression of arson." (Section 488.2)
- "The prevention of fires or danger to life or property therefrom" (Section 488.3)

**Why:** To track all fire suppression, investigation, and prevention activities, which are core agency missions.

**One row represents:** A single fire or emergency incident requiring department response.

**Covers:** All fire suppression, fire investigation, arson suppression, and fire prevention responses.

**Columns:**
- Incident ID
- Incident Type (e.g., Structure Fire, Vehicle Fire, Brush Fire, Arson Investigation, Prevention Response)
- Incident Date and Time
- Incident Location (Address, Vessel Name/ID, Dock/Pier ID, Coordinates)
- Reporting Party Information
- Initial Call Details
- Response Units (linked to Dataset 8: Fire Department Equipment Inventory, Dataset 6: Employee Roster & Assignments)
- Arrival Date and Time
- Incident Controlled Date and Time
- Incident Clear Date and Time
- Casualties (Injuries, Fatalities)
- Property Damage Estimate
- Cause of Fire (if determined)
- Arson Suspected (Yes/No)
- Investigation Status
- Investigator(s) (linked to Dataset 6: Employee Roster & Assignments)
- Referral to other departments (Yes/No, linked to Dataset 15: Inter-Agency Referrals)
- Notes/Summary

**9. Fire Department Facility & Unit Status Changes**

**Quotes:**
- "the commissioner shall provide written notice with supporting documentation at least forty-five days prior to the permanent closing of any firehouse or the permanent removal or relocation of any fire fighting unit to the council members, community boards and borough presidents whose districts are served by such facility or unit and the chairperson of the council's public safety committee." - "The four months during which the written notice is effective shall be tolled for any period in which a restraining order or injunction prohibiting the closing of such noticed facility or unit shall be in effect." (Section 487.a)
