**Identified Tabular Datasets**

**1. Emergency Food Delivery Program Termination Record**

**Quotes:**
- "§ 1-01 Emergency Food Delivery. [Terminated*]"
- "* Editor's note: Subsection e. of 72 RCNY § 1-01 provided: "The program provided for in this section will terminate at the end of the COVID-19 public health emergency, as determined by Order of the Mayor, or on such other date as determined by the Commissioner of Emergency Management or such other person as may be designated to succeed such Commissioner in managing the program provided for in this section". Executive Order 126, declaring the public health emergency, ended on June 20, 2023."

**Why:** The termination of a program is a legal event requiring formal documentation and tracking for compliance.

**One row represents:** The official record of the Emergency Food Delivery Program's termination.

**Covers:** The formal cessation of the Emergency Food Delivery Program.

**Columns:**
- Program Name
- Program Start Date
- Program End Date
- Termination Date (Official)
- Termination Authority (e.g., Executive Order 126)
- Commissioner's Determination Date
- Reason for Termination
- Record Retention Policy Applied

**2. Emergency Food Delivery Records (Historical)**

**Quotes:**
- "§ 1-01 Emergency Food Delivery. [Terminated*]"
- "* Editor's note: Subsection e. of 72 RCNY § 1-01 provided: "The program provided for in this section will terminate at the end of the COVID-19 public health emergency..."

**Why:** To fulfill its mission during its active period, the agency needed to track individual food deliveries for accountability and reporting.

**One row represents:** A single food delivery event made under the program.

**Covers:** All individual food deliveries made during the active period of the Emergency Food Delivery Program.

**Columns:**
- Delivery ID
- **Recipient ID** (links to Dataset 3)
- Delivery Date
- Delivery Time
- Delivery Address
- Food Items Delivered
- Quantity Delivered
- Delivery Status (e.g., delivered, attempted, cancelled)
- Driver/Volunteer ID
- Delivery Vehicle Information
- Program Phase (e.g., initial, amended)

**3. Emergency Food Delivery Program Participant Records (Historical)**

**Quotes:**
- "§ 1-01 Emergency Food Delivery. [Terminated*]"
- "* Editor's note: Subsection e. of 72 RCNY § 1-01 provided: "The program provided for in this section will terminate at the end of the COVID-19 public health emergency..."

**Why:** To manage and account for services provided, the agency would have tracked individuals or households receiving food assistance.

**One row represents:** A single participant (individual or household) registered in the program.

**Covers:** All individuals or households registered to receive food assistance through the Emergency Food Delivery Program during its active period.

**Columns:**
- Participant ID
- Name/Household Name
- Primary Contact Name
- Primary Contact Phone Number
- Primary Contact Email
- Address
- Eligibility Criteria Met
- Enrollment Date
- Program Status (e.g., active, inactive, suspended)
- Number of Household Members
- Special Dietary Needs

**4. Emergency Food Delivery Program Vendor/Partner Records (Historical)**

**Quotes:**
- "§ 1-01 Emergency Food Delivery. [Terminated*]"
- "* Editor's note: Subsection e. of 72 RCNY § 1-01 provided: "The program provided for in this section will terminate at the end of the COVID-19 public health emergency..."

**Why:** To operate the program, the agency would have engaged with vendors or partners, requiring tracking of these relationships and transactions.

**One row represents:** A single vendor or partner organization involved in the program.

**Covers:** All vendors, suppliers, or partner organizations that provided services or goods to the Emergency Food Delivery Program during its active period.

**Columns:**
- Vendor/Partner ID
- Organization Name
- Contact Person
- Contact Information
- Contract Start Date
- Contract End Date
- Services/Goods Provided
- Contract Value
- Payment Status
- Performance Notes

**Non-Tabular Information:**

- Rules and Regulations (e.g., 72 RCNY § 1-01 text itself)
- Executive Orders (e.g., Executive Order 126)
- Legal opinions or interpretations regarding program termination
- Internal policy documents related to program operation or termination
- Any research documents or reports generated during the program's active phase
- Photos or other media related to food delivery operations
