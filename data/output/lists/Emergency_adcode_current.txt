*   **One row represents:** A single piece of feedback or a complaint.
*   **Covers:** Feedback specifically related to cooling centers, but can be generalized to other emergency services.
*   **Columns:** Feedback ID, Date/Time Received, Source (e.g., "Website," "Phone," "In-person"), Feedback Type (e.g., "Complaint," "Suggestion," "Praise"), Related Service/Facility (e.g., "Cooling Center," "Shelter"), **Facility ID (linked to Dataset 3, if applicable)**, Description of Feedback, Obstacles Reported, Resolution/Action Taken, Notes.
