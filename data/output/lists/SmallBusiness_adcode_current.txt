**1. Small Business Legal Service Cases**

**Quotes:**
*   "Subject to appropriation, the commissioner shall provide, at no cost to a covered small business tenant, out-of-court legal assistance and may provide, at no cost to a covered small business tenant, in-court legal representation." (§ 22-1006.b.1)
*   "The number of small businesses that received such legal services in the previous fiscal year, disaggregated by the following characteristics of such businesses: (a) Borough and postal code; (b) Number of persons employed by business; (c) Net income of business; and (d) Type of business as categorized by section 121.201 of subpart A of part 121 of title 13 of the code of federal regulations;" (§ 22-1006.c.1)
*   "A description of the aggregate performance outcomes of designated organizations, information on the geographic areas for which such organizations provided legal services, and identification of the types of covered legal matters handled by designated organizations." (§ 22-1006.c.2)
*   "Covered legal matter. The term "covered legal matter" means a legal matter related to a commercial lease, including but not limited to drafting and negotiation of lease terms, terminating a lease, and addressing other lease-related issues." (§ 22-1006.a)
*   "Covered proceeding. The term "covered proceeding" means any proceeding in any court of competent jurisdiction against a covered small business tenant to: (i) Evict such tenant... (ii) Terminate such tenancy; or (iii) Establish a breach of the terms of such tenant's commercial lease." (§ 22-1006.a)
*   "Covered small business tenant. The term "covered small business tenant" means a business that is: (i) A lessee or sublessee in a commercial tenancy located in the city; (ii) Small, in accordance with the size standards set forth in section 121.201 of title 13 of the code of federal regulations; (iii) Not a franchisee... and (iv) Owned and operated by one or more persons, all of whom are income-eligible individuals." (§ 22-1006.a)

**Why:** To fulfill the mandate of providing legal services and reporting on their provision and outcomes, individual cases must be tracked.

**One row represents:** A single instance of legal assistance or representation provided to a covered small business tenant.

**Covers:** All legal services (out-of-court assistance and in-court representation) provided to eligible small businesses.

**Columns:**
*   Legal Service Case ID
*   Small Business ID
*   Small Business Name
*   Small Business Address (Street, **Borough**, **Postal Code**)
*   Number of Persons Employed by Business
*   Net Income of Business
*   Type of Business (e.g., NAICS code from 13 CFR 121.201)
*   Is Business a Franchisee? (Yes/No)
*   Are Owners Income-Eligible? (Yes/No)
*   Legal Service Type (Out-of-Court Assistance, In-Court Representation)
*   Covered Legal Matter Type (e.g., Lease Drafting, Lease Negotiation, Lease Termination, Eviction Proceeding, Breach of Lease Proceeding)
*   Date Service Initiated
*   Date Service Concluded
*   Designated Organization ID (linked to Dataset 8)
*   Outcome of Service (e.g., Lease renegotiated, Eviction avoided, Case dismissed, Ongoing)
*   Geographic Area of Service Provision (as reported by designated organization)
