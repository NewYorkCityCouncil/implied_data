**1. Driver/Chauffeur Licenses**

**Quotes:**
*   "standards for driver safety" (Section 2300)
*   "set standards and criteria for the licensing of vehicles, drivers and chauffeurs, owners and operators engaged in such services" (Section 2300)
*   "The issuance, revocation, suspension of licenses for drivers, chauffeurs, owners or operators of vehicles, other than licenses issued pursuant to state law, and for taxicab brokers and the establishment of qualifying standards required for such licensees." (Section 2303.b.5)
*   "Compile and report statistics relating to driver demographics, disaggregated by ethnicity or race, gender, disability and other categories as appropriate" (Section 2305.c.1)

**Why:** To regulate individuals operating for-hire vehicles, the agency must track their licenses and associated information.

**One row represents:** A single driver or chauffeur license.

**Covers:** All licenses issued, revoked, or suspended for drivers and chauffeurs.

**Columns:**
*   License ID
*   Driver/Chauffeur Name
*   License Type (e.g., Taxi, Limousine, Commuter Van)
*   License Status (Active, Suspended, Revoked, Expired)
*   Issue Date
*   Expiration Date
*   Date of Last Renewal
*   Driver Demographics (Ethnicity/Race, Gender, Disability Status, other categories)
*   Qualifying Standards Met (e.g., background check status, driving record check)
*   Associated **Owner/Operator License ID** (Dataset 7)
*   Associated **Vehicle License ID** (Dataset 2)

**3. Enforcement Actions**

**Quotes:**
*   "enforcement actions undertaken by the commission, whether the enforcement action was dismissed or settled, or if a penalty was imposed by the commission on the subject of the enforcement action." (Section 2302.a)
*   "The information regarding enforcement actions shall include, but not be limited to, enforcement actions relating to illegal street hails, unlicensed vehicles, overcharging, and toll lane infractions." (Section 2302.a)
*   "Enforcement actions shall be further disaggregated by the subject matter of the action and geographic location." (Section 2302.a)
*   "charges of violations of the provisions of the administrative code and rules promulgated thereunder shall be adjudicated by the administrative tribunal established by the commission" (Section 2303.a)
*   "The commission shall create an administrative tribunal to adjudicate charges of violation" (Section 2303.c.1)
*   "The commission shall have the power to enforce its tribunal's decisions and orders imposing civil penalties, not to exceed ten thousand dollars for each respondent, for violations relating to unlicensed vehicles for hire and unlicensed drivers of vehicles for hire and for violations relating to the operation of commuter van services without authorization and the operation of unlicensed commuter vans and unlicensed drivers of commuter vans" (Section 2303.c.1)
*   "Any such decision or order of the commission's administrative tribunal imposing a civil penalty... shall constitute a judgment rendered by the commission" (Section 2303.c.1)
*   "Before a judgment based upon a default may be so entered the commission or administrative tribunal shall have first notified the respondent by first class mail" (Section 2303.c.1)
*   "the notice of violation shall have been served in the same manner as is prescribed for service of process" (Section 2303.c.2)

**Why:** To track all violations, adjudications, penalties, and license actions, which are critical for public safety and mandated reporting.

**One row represents:** A single enforcement action (e.g., a violation notice, a tribunal adjudication, a license suspension/revocation).

**Covers:** All stages of enforcement, from initial violation notice to final judgment and penalty imposition, including license revocations/suspensions.

**Columns:**
*   Enforcement Action ID
*   Date of Violation
*   Date of Notice of Violation
*   Violation Type (e.g., Illegal Street Hail, Unlicensed Vehicle, Overcharging, Toll Lane Infraction, Unlicensed Driver, Service Refusal)
*   Subject of Action (Person/Entity)
*   Subject **License ID** (can link to Dataset 1, 2, 7, or 8)
*   Subject License Type
*   Geographic Location of Violation (e.g., Borough, Coordinates)
*   Enforcement Officer ID
*   Date of Tribunal Adjudication
*   Adjudication Outcome (Dismissed, Settled, Penalty Imposed, License Action)
*   Penalty Amount (if applicable)
*   Judgment Entry Date (if applicable)
*   License Action Taken (Suspension, Revocation, etc.)
*   Duration of License Action (if applicable)
*   Date Notice of Default Mailed (if applicable)
*   Date Notice of Violation Served
*   Method of Service (e.g., First Class Mail, Personal Service)
*   Associated **Complaint ID** (Dataset 4, if initiated by a complaint)

**9. Fare Rate Schedules**

**Quotes:**
*   "establish certain rates" (Section 2300)
