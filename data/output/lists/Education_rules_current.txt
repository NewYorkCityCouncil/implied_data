**Identified Tabular Datasets**

1.  **Employee Roster**
    *   **Quotes:**
        *   "employee timecards" (from instructions)
        *   "ensuring specialists are up to date on required certifications" (from instructions)
        *   "Staff Attendance Count" (from example Dataset 4: Senior Center Staff Training Events)

        *   "Inspector ID" (from example Dataset 2: Child Care Provider Home Inspections)
    *   **Why:** Fundamental for managing human resources, payroll, and tracking staff involvement across all agency operations.
    *   **One row represents:** A single employee of the agency.
    *   **Covers:** All individuals employed by the agency.
    *   **Columns:**
        *   Employee ID
        *   First Name
        *   Last Name
        *   Job Title
        *   Department
        *   Hire Date
        *   Employment Status (Active, Leave, Terminated)
        *   Contact Information (Email, Phone)
        *   Supervisor ID (linked to Employee ID)
