**Data Systems for Agency Legal Requirements**

**1. Test Catalog**

*   **Quotes:**
    *   "a report relating to each test administered and disclosed pursuant to section three hundred forty-two of this article" (§ 341.4.a)
    *   "each question used in calculating the subject's raw score on each test form administered" (§ 341.5.a)
    *   "Disclosure of test contents." (§ 342)
    *   "the dates of each test administered by the test agency during the testing year" (§ 342.9.a)
*   **Why:** To manage and track all standardized tests the agency is responsible for administering and reporting on.
*   **One row represents:** A specific standardized test offered by the agency.
*   **Covers:** All standardized tests administered by the test agency.
*   **Columns:**
    *   Test ID
    *   Test Name
    *   Test Type (e.g., SAT, GRE General Test, MCAT, TOEFL, SAT Subject Test)
    *   Sponsoring Agency/Manufacturer
    *   Description of Test Purpose (from § 343.1.a)

**4. Test Subject Question Responses**

*   **Quotes:**
    *   "Each such item report shall show for each question used in calculating the test subject's raw score, the item's content area specification, and where disclosed pursuant to section three hundred forty-two of this article, information identifying the text of the item, and the correct answer-rates and R-biserial correlations for all test subjects, combined and separately by race, ethnicity, gender or linguistic background as reported under subdivision two of this section." (§ 341.5.b)
    *   "Correct answer-rates shall be calculated by dividing the number of correct answers for a question used in calculating the subject's raw score on all test forms administered and disclosed pursuant to section three hundred forty-two of this article... by the number of test subjects to whom those test forms were administered." (§ 341.5.b)
    *   "R-biserial correlations shall be calculated by correlating performance on a question used in calculating the subject's raw score on each test form administered... with performance on the tests as shown by total scores." (§ 341.5.b)
    *   "a copy of the test subject's answer sheet, or answer record where there is no answer sheet" (§ 342.2.b)
*   **Why:** To store the raw, incident-level data of each test subject's response to individual questions, necessary for detailed item analysis and reporting.
*   **One row represents:** A single test subject's response to a specific question on a test.
*   **Covers:** All individual question responses from test subjects on administered tests.
*   **Columns:**
    *   Test Subject ID (links to Dataset 3)
    *   Test Administration ID (links to Dataset 2)
    *   Test Item ID (links to Dataset 5)
    *   Response Provided (e.g., A, B, C, D, or text for open-ended)
    *   Is Correct (Yes/No)

    *   "Each such item report shall show for each question used in calculating the test subject's raw score, the item's content area specification, and where disclosed pursuant to section three hundred forty-two of this article, information identifying the text of the item, and the correct answer-rates and R-biserial correlations for all test subjects, combined and separately by race, ethnicity, gender or linguistic background as reported under subdivision two of this section." **9. Test Score Authenticity Investigations**

*   **Quotes:**
    *   "Each test agency shall ensure due process protection of test subjects whose scores are being questioned for suspected inauthenticity or irregularity in test administration." (§ 344-b)
    *   "When a test agency is presented with information which renders the test subject's test score suspect... the test agency is responsible for reviewing the information and determining if withholding the test subject's score is warranted." (§ 344-b.1)
    *   "If the test agency determines that withholding the test subject's score is warranted, the test agency shall give the test subject written notice of the test agency's decision." (§ 344-b.2)
    *   "The notice to the test subject shall include all of the following: (a) a complete summary of the information submitted... (b) a complete summary of the pertinent facts... (c) a statement of the test subject's right to receive... details... (d) the policies and procedures... (e) the potential consequences... (f) a summary of the information that may be submitted... (g) a statement informing the test subject that the opportunity to respond... for not more than fifteen working days" (§ 344-b.3)
    *   "After the time period... has expired, the test agency shall review all of the evidence and shall make a good faith effort to render a decision... within fifteen working days and notify the test subject immediately." (§ 344-b.4)
    *   "When the test agency determines that substantial evidence exists to support cancellation or invalidation of a test score, the test agency shall provide the test subject with a choice of the following options: (a) cancellation of the test scores in question, with full refund of all test fees; (b) an opportunity to take the test again privately and without charge; or (c) an opportunity to seek arbitration or judicial review of the matter." (§ 344-b.5)
    *   "The test subject shall have thirty days following receipt of the notice by registered mail to respond" (§ 344-b.6)
    *   "If the test subject responds... the test agency shall review the contents of the response and comply with one of the following: (a) If the test subject requests cancellation... full refund... (b) If the test subject requests an opportunity to take the test again... the test agency shall make appropriate accommodations... If the score on the retest is higher, the higher score shall be reported." (§ 344-b.7)
    *   "The test agency shall not release confidential information to any authorized test score recipients regarding a test subject under pending investigation, unless authorized to do so by the test subject." (§ 344-b.8)
    *   "The test agency shall immediately release the test score... where substantial evidence does not exist to render the inauthenticity or irregularity of the test score." (§ 344-b.9)
*   **Why:** To track all investigations into suspected inauthenticity or irregularity of test scores, ensuring due process and proper resolution.
*   **One row represents:** An investigation into a test subject's score authenticity.
*   **Covers:** All instances where a test subject's score is questioned for authenticity or irregularity.
*   **Columns:**
    *   Investigation ID
    *   Test Subject ID (links to Dataset 3)
    *   Test Administration ID (links to Dataset 2)
    *   Date Information Received
    *   Reason for Suspicion (e.g., collusion, cheating, irregular administration, statistical data)
    *   Date Decision to Withhold Score
    *   Notice Sent Date (via registered mail)
    *   Test Subject Response Deadline
    *   Test Subject Response Date
    *   Final Decision Date
    *   Decision Outcome (e.g., Score Validated, Score Canceled, Retest Offered, Arbitration/Judicial Review)
    *   Option Chosen by Subject (if applicable: Cancellation, Retest, Arbitration/Judicial Review)
    *   Refund Amount (if applicable)
    *   Retest Administration ID (if applicable, links to Dataset 2)
    *   Retest Score (if applicable)
    *   Score Release Status (Released, Withheld)

**Non-Tabular Information:**

*   **Test Registration Forms:** While containing data, the form itself is a document. The data is extracted into tabular datasets (e.g., Test Subject Performance Records).
*   **Test Answer Sheets/Records:** Raw input documents for test subject responses. The data is extracted into tabular datasets (e.g., Test Subject Question Responses).
*   **Rules for Converting Raw Scores:** Policy documents explaining scoring methodology.
*   **Explanation of Scoring Rules:** Explanatory documents for test subjects.
*   **Test Reports (Aggregated):** Reports showing mean-scaled scores, standard deviations, and score distributions by demographic groups (§ 341.4.b). These are outputs derived from tabular data, not the raw tabular data itself.
*   **Item Reports (Aggregated):** Reports showing correct answer-rates, R-biserial correlations, and differential item functioning data (§ 341.5.b, d). These are outputs derived from tabular data.
*   **Documentation of Differential Item Functioning Calculation Methods:** Explanatory documents for the methodology used (§ 341.5.d).
*   **Copies of Test Questions:** The actual text/images of test questions (§ 342.1.a).
*   **Corresponding Acceptable Answers:** The actual answers for test questions (§ 342.1.b).
*   **Test Subject's Answer Sheet/Record:** The physical or digital record of a test subject's responses (§ 342.2.b).
*   **Correct Answer Sheet:** The master answer key for a test (§ 342.2.b).
*   **Test Registration Form (for disclosure election):** A specific form for subjects to elect disclosure (§ 342.2).
*   **Correspondence Challenging Test Questions:** The actual letters/emails from test subjects or institutions (§ 342.8.a).
*   **Test Agency's Written Responses to Challenges:** The actual letters/emails from the agency (§ 342.8.b).
*   **Information Provided with Registration Form or Score Report:** Statements on test purposes, score interpretation, reporting methods, agency promises, privacy policies, retention policies, disclosure access, test section descriptions, accommodations for disabled subjects, administration procedures, score use recommendations, additional information sources, test subject rights, and fairness procedures (§ 343.1). These are informational documents.
*   **Study on Effects of Pretesting:** A research document (§ 344-a).
*   **Written Notice of Score Withholding:** The formal letter sent to a test subject whose score is suspect (§ 344-b.2, 3).
*   **Commissioner's Regulations:** Regulations promulgated to implement the article (§ 345).
*   **Computerized Test Review Process Report:** A report detailing the implementation of the review process for computerized tests (§ 346).
*   **Advisory Committee Study Design:** A document outlining the plan for data analysis (§ 346-a.3).
*   **Advisory Committee Reports to State Legislature:** Findings and recommendations documents (§ 346-a.5).
*   **Textbook Catalogs/Bulletins (College):** Informational documents for applicants (§ 343.4).
*   **Sample Test Forms (SAT Subject Tests):** Representative test documents (§ 342.5-b).
*   **Updated Information (SAT Subject Tests):** Descriptions and sample items for new/changed tests (§ 342.5-b).
*   **Guidelines (Instructional Materials for Disabled Students):** Information, training, technical guidance, and best practices documents (§ 715.1.h).
