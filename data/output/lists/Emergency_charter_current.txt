**1. Emergency Incident Log**

**Quotes:**
- "a. coordinate the city's response to all emergency conditions and potential incidents which require a multi-agency response, including but not limited to severe weather, threats from natural hazards and natural disasters, power and other public service outages, labor unrest other than the keeping of the peace, water main breaks, transportation and transit incidents, hazardous substance discharges, building collapses, aviation disasters, explosions, acts of terrorism and such other emergency conditions and incidents which affect public health and safety;" (Section 497.a)
- "g. operate an emergency operations center to assist the city in managing emergency conditions and potential incidents that may require a multi-agency response;" (Section 497.g)
- "498.a. The department shall be the lead agency in the coordination and facilitation of resources in incidents involving public safety and health, including incidents which may involve acts of terrorism." (Section 498.a)

**Why:** To effectively coordinate and manage emergency responses, the agency must track each incident's details and status.

**One row represents:** A single emergency condition or incident requiring a multi-agency response.

**Covers:** All emergency conditions and potential incidents that require multi-agency response, as defined in the charter.

**Columns:**
- Incident ID
- Incident Name/Title
- Incident Type (e.g., Severe Weather, Natural Disaster, Power Outage, Water Main Break, Transportation Incident, Hazardous Substance Discharge, Building Collapse, Aviation Disaster, Explosion, Act of Terrorism, Other)
- Incident Sub-Type (if applicable)
- Date/Time Reported
- Date/Time Incident Start
- Date/Time Incident End
- Geographic Location (Address, Coordinates, Borough, etc.)
- Initial Severity Assessment
- Current Severity Level
- Status (e.g., Reported, Active, Contained, Resolved, Post-Incident Review)
- Lead Agency (if not EMD)
- Agencies Involved (list of agency IDs)
- Key Contact Person for Incident
- Description of Incident
- Impact on Public Health and Safety
- Mayoral Emergency Order Issued (Yes/No)
- Date/Time Mayoral Order Issued
- Incident Commander ID (links to Dataset 13)
- Incident Commander Name
- Date/Time Last Update
