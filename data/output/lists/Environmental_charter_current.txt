**1. Water Infrastructure Assets**

**Quotes:**
*   "The commissioner shall have charge and control of: (a) All structures and property connected with the supply and distribution of water for public use not owned by private corporations, including all fire and drinking hydrants and all water meters;" Section 1403.a.(1)(a)
*   "Furnishing the water supply and maintaining its quality, and of the investigation for and the construction of all works necessary to deliver the proper and required quality of water with ample reserve for contingencies and future demands;" Section 1403.a.(1)(b)

**Why:** To manage and maintain the city's water supply infrastructure, the agency must track all related assets.

**One row represents:** A single piece of water infrastructure or property.

**Covers:** All structures, property, fire hydrants, drinking hydrants, water meters, and other works connected with the public water supply and distribution.

**Columns:**
*   Water Infrastructure Asset ID
*   Asset Type (e.g., Fire Hydrant, Drinking Hydrant, Water Meter, Pumping Station, Reservoir, Water Main, Treatment Plant, Valve)
*   Location (Address, GIS Coordinates)
*   Installation Date
*   Manufacturer/Model
*   Capacity/Size (e.g., pipe diameter, meter flow rate)
*   Status (e.g., Active, Inactive, Under Repair)
*   Last Maintenance Date (links to Dataset 3: Maintenance Activities)
*   Last Inspection Date (links to Dataset 4: Environmental Inspections and Monitoring Events)
*   Ownership (City, Private Corporation)
*   Associated Water Supply System ID

**4. Environmental Inspections and Monitoring Events**

**Quotes:**
*   "The commissioner shall examine into the sources of water supply of any private companies supplying the city or any portion thereof or its inhabitants with water to see that the same is wholesome and the supply is adequate, to establish such rules and regulations in respect thereof as are reasonable and necessary for the convenience of the public and to exercise superintendence, regulation and control in respect to the supply of water by such water companies." Section 1403.a.(2)
*   "With regard to community private sewage disposal systems, the commissioner shall have the authority to perform inspections, and to issue notices of violation for violations of any provision of the New York city health code relating to private sewage disposal..." Section 1403.b.(1)
*   "The commissioner shall have the power to administer and enforce provisions of law, rules and regulations relating to the management and control of discharges and runoff from public and private property, including but not limited to stormwater discharges; regulate and control discharges into water within and about the city of New York of harmful or objectionable substances, contaminants and pollutants that may have an adverse impact on waters of the state; enforce all laws, rules and regulations with respect to discharges described in this paragraph; make such investigations and studies as may be desirable for the purpose of such enforcement and of controlling and eliminating pollution of waters within and about the city of New York;" Section 1403.b-1.(1)
*   "The commissioner shall regulate and control the emission into the open air of harmful or objectionable substances, including, but not limited to, smoke, soot, dust, fumes, flyash, gas vapors, odors and any products of combustion or incomplete combustion resulting from the use of fuel burning equipment or from the heating of fuels or refuse. The commissioner shall enforce all laws, rules and regulations with respect to such emissions and shall make such investigations and studies as may be desirable for the purpose of such enforcement and of controlling and eliminating air pollution..." Section 1403.c.
*   "The commissioner shall enforce all laws, rules and regulations to eliminate noise pollution. The commissioner shall make investigations and studies to develop permissible sound levels and to correct problems related to noise control..." Section 1403.d.

**Why:** To fulfill responsibilities for water quality, sewage, and pollution control, the agency must track all inspections and monitoring activities.

**One row represents:** A single inspection or monitoring event.

**Covers:** Inspections of private water companies, private sewage disposal systems (community and non-community), monitoring of water discharges/runoff, air emissions, and noise levels.

**Columns:**
*   Inspection/Monitoring Event ID
*   Date of Event
*   Time of Event
*   Inspector ID (links to Dataset 10: Employee Certifications and Training)
*   Inspection Type (e.g., Water Quality, Private Water Company, Private Sewage System, Community Private Sewage System, Stormwater Discharge, Air Emission, Noise Level)
*   Location (Address, GIS Coordinates)
*   Regulated Entity ID (e.g., Private Water Company ID, Private Sewage System Permit ID (links to Dataset 5: Private Sewage Disposal System Permits), Facility ID)
*   Parameters Measured (e.g., pollutant levels, sound levels, flow rates)
*   Measurement Results/Observations
*   Compliance Status (e.g., In Compliance, Violation Found, Warning Issued)
*   Reference to Violation ID (links to Dataset 6: Notices of Violation and Enforcement Actions)
*   Equipment Used (links to Dataset 11: Departmental Equipment Inventory)

*   **Legal Documents:** Court orders, administrative tribunal decisions, and other legal instruments related to enforcement actions or cost recovery. (Section 1403.h.(3))
*   **Emergency Response Plans:** Detailed plans for responding to releases or threatened releases of hazardous substances. (Section 1403.h)
*   **Energy Policy Documents:** Formulated energy policies for the city. (Section 1403.g)
