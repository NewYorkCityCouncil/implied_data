- "manage and superintend all real property acquired by the city for, or devoted to, housing or urban renewal purposes" Section 1802.6(c)
- "manage and superintend all residential real property of the city not used for public purposes" Section 1802.6(k)
- "exercise such other powers and duties as may be prescribed by law in relation to the management, demolition or sealing or other treatment of residential real property of the city" Section 1802.6(l)
- "enforce the provisions of the zoning resolution related to eligibility requirements with respect to the development, acquisition, rehabilitation, preservation, sale or rental of affordable housing administered by the department pursuant to such resolution" Section 1802.6(n)
- "enter and inspect any building, structure, enclosure, premises or any part thereof" Section 1803.2
- "Project boundary. The term "project boundary" means the perimeter of an urban renewal area." Section 1806.1
- "Urban renewal site. The term "urban renewal site" means a tract of land that is designated for acquisition for one or more particular land uses and identified as a redevelopment parcel pursuant to an urban renewal plan." Section 1806.1
- "The department shall post on such website the following information or links to such information about each such urban renewal area, to the extent that such information is available in department or city records: (d) The project boundary of such urban renewal area; (e) The designated urban renewal sites within the project boundary of such urban renewal area and the permitted uses of such designated urban renewal sites pursuant to such urban renewal plan;" Section 1806.3
- "homeowner” means a natural person who utilizes a building within the city as a primary residence by being: (i) the shareholder in a cooperative corporation that entitles such owner to occupancy of a dwelling unit in such building; (ii) the owner of a residential condominium unit in such building; or (iii) the owner of such building containing a residence within the city, provided such building has between 1 and 4 dwelling units." Section 1807.a

**Why:** The agency's core mission revolves around residential properties, requiring a comprehensive inventory for all related activities.

**One row represents:** A single residential building or property unit within the city.

**Covers:** All residential buildings and properties relevant to the department's functions, including privately owned, city-owned, and those within urban renewal areas.

**Columns:**
- Property ID (Unique Identifier)
- Address (Street, City, State, Zip)
- Borough
- Community District
- Property Type (e.g., Multi-family, Single-family, Condo, Co-op)
- Number of Dwelling Units
- Ownership Type (Private, City-owned, Publicly-aided)
- Current Occupancy Status
- Zoning District
- Year Built
- Last Major Renovation Date
- Status (e.g., Active, Demolished, Sealed)
- Urban Renewal Area ID (linked to Dataset 7)
- Urban Renewal Site ID (linked to Dataset 9)
- Homeowner Advocate Eligibility (Yes/No)
