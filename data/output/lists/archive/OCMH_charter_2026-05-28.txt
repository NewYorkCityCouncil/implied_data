1.  **Agency Strategies and Proposals**

    **Quotes:**
    *   "Develop and support the implementation of strategies to close gaps in mental health care identified by the office established pursuant to this section by: (a) Monitoring the implementation of such proposals;" (c.2)
    *   "Effectively and equitably promote mental health crisis prevention, intervention and stabilization practices;" (c.3.a)
    *   "Promote mental health screening;" (c.3.b)
    *   "Facilitate referrals to mental health care;" (c.3.c)
    *   "Offer training;" (c.3.d)
    *   "Implement other strategies to promote mental health." (c.3.e)
    *   "Developing and implementing strategic partnerships with other agencies and entities to increase access to mental health care;" (c.4.a)
    *   "Disseminating resources to enhance mental health literacy, promote access to mental health care, and promote equity in access to treatment." (c.4.b)

    **Why:** To track the lifecycle, progress, and outcomes of each strategy and proposal developed and implemented by the agency.

    **One row represents:** A single strategy or proposal developed or supported by the agency.

    **Covers:** All strategies and proposals aimed at reducing substance misuse, promoting access to mental health treatment, promoting equity, reducing disparities, preventing crises, and closing care gaps.

    **Columns:**
    *   Strategy ID
    *   Strategy Name
    *   Description
    *   Objective (e.g., Reduce Substance Misuse, Promote Access, Reduce Disparity, Crisis Prevention)
    *   Start Date
    *   Planned End Date
    *   Actual End Date
    *   Status (e.g., Planned, In Progress, Implemented, Completed, On Hold, Canceled)
    *   Lead Department/Team
    *   Related Interagency Policy ID (linked to Dataset 4)
    *   Related Partnership ID (linked to Dataset 5)
    *   Monitoring Notes
    *   Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
    *   Outcome Summary

3.  **Annual Report Research Activities**

    **Quotes:**

(g)
    *   "To identify such gaps, such office may review existing data and research, conduct research as needed, and interview agency staff, community partners, mental health providers and other relevant experts." (g)
