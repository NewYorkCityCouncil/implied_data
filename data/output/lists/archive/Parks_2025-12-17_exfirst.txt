**Section 1, Mission Summary:**

The Department of Parks and Recreation is responsible for the management, care, and improvement of New York City's public parks, squares, playgrounds, recreation properties, and related facilities, including natural areas, beaches, pools, and marinas. Its key programs involve maintaining public spaces, providing recreational and educational opportunities, enforcing rules and regulations, and overseeing capital projects and environmental initiatives. The agency significantly impacts constituents by enhancing their health, welfare, recreation, and overall quality of life through accessible and well-maintained public green spaces and recreational services.

**Section 4, Non-tabular Data Summary:**

The agency would maintain a significant amount of non-tabular information to fulfill its legal requirements and operational needs. This includes:

*   **Application Forms:** All original paper or digital application forms for permits, memberships, programs, waivers, employment, and waitlists.
*   **Rules and Regulations:** The complete text of the City Charter, Administrative Code, and Agency Rules, along with any internal policies, guidelines, and manuals (e.g., stormwater retention manual, native biodiversity design manual, tree removal protocol, inspection criteria, load-in plans for events).
*   **Contracts and Agreements:** Full legal documents for all contracts, leases, and agreements with third parties (e.g., USTA, Zoological Society, historical societies, conservancies, construction contractors), including any attachments or amendments.
*   **Financial Records:** Receipts for fees, fines, payments, and reimbursements; detailed budget documents; audit reports; financial statements.
*   **Correspondence:** Official letters, emails, and memoranda related to permit applications, appeals, rule variances, public complaints, incident reports, inter-agency coordination, and legal matters.
*   **Maps and Plans:** Architectural plans, engineering drawings for capital projects, landscape designs, zoning maps, official maps of parks and facilities, maps of concession boundaries, designated quiet zones, emergency evacuation plans.
*   **Photographs and Video:** Visual documentation of park conditions, amenities, construction progress, damages, incidents, rule violations, special events, and GreenThumb gardens. Filming and photography permits would also generate such media.
*   **Research Documents:** Studies, evaluations, and reports on environmental conditions (e.g., tree health methodologies, surfacing material assessments, urban forest plans), recreational needs, and public safety.
*   **Training Materials:** Curriculum, manuals, and presentations for staff training (e.g., lifeguard safety, AED operation, elder abuse detection for senior service providers, wildfire mitigation).
*   **Public Outreach Materials:** Brochures, flyers, website content, signage (content, not the inventory itself), press releases, and social media posts for public education, event promotion, and notifications.
*   **Meeting Minutes:** Records of official meetings for the NYC Sports Commission, conservancy boards, and internal departmental meetings.
*   **Legal Documents:** Court orders, subpoenas, legal advisories, administrative hearing decisions, and other case-related files for violations and other legal proceedings.
*   **Employee Records:** Personnel files, certifications (e.g., USCG captain's license, lifeguard certifications), training records (e.g., AED training, wildfire mitigation training).
*   **Environmental Reports:** Detailed water quality reports from the Department of Health and Mental Hygiene, environmental impact statements, and other assessments.
*   **LiDAR Data:** Raw light detection and ranging data collected for urban forest canopy assessment (the raw data, distinct from the processed tabular canopy coverage).
