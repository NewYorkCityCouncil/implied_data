**Identified Tabular Datasets**

1.  **Telecommunications Franchises and Revocable Consents**

    **Quotes:**
    *   "to administer all franchises and revocable consents relating to telecommunications pursuant to the provisions of chapter fourteen, including, without limitation, proposing authorizing resolutions for telecommunications franchises, developing and issuing requests for proposals or other solicitations of proposals for telecommunications franchises, selecting telecommunications franchisees, reviewing and approving petitions for revocable consents relating to telecommunications, negotiating the terms of contracts or other agreements relating to telecommunications franchises and revocable consents, and enforcing the terms and conditions of such agreements;" (Section 1072.c)

    **Why:** To administer, track, and enforce telecommunications agreements, the agency needs to maintain detailed records of each franchise and consent.

    **One row represents:** A single telecommunications franchise or revocable consent agreement.

    **Covers:** All active, pending, and historical telecommunications franchises and revocable consents.

    **Columns:**
    *   Agreement ID
    *   Agreement Type (Franchise, Revocable Consent)
    *   Franchisee/Permittee Name
    *   Legal Entity ID
    *   Application Date
    *   Approval Date
    *   Start Date
    *   End Date
    *   Status (Active, Expired, Revoked, Pending)
    *   Terms and Conditions Summary
    *   Enforcement Actions (linked to Dataset 11)
    *   Related RFP ID
    *   Resolution ID
    *   Geographic Scope
    *   Fees/Payments

7.  **Permitted Street Closures**

    **Quotes:**
    *   "to provide to the public at no charge on the city's website an interactive map, updated as often as practicable and necessary but not less than once per week, displaying the following: 1. Permitted and approved street closures that do not allow for the passage of vehicular traffic on that street, including but not limited to closures for special events, crane operations and other construction work, film shoots and paving operations; and 2. Parking regulations. The information related to paragraph (1) of this subdivision shall be searchable and sortable by time, date and borough, except that street closures for crane operations, construction work and paving operations shall have the notation "subject to closure" during times where closure has been permitted and approved but where such closure may or may not occur on a particular day. All information required by this subdivision shall be available on the city's website as soon as practicable but in no case less than one week prior to any such closure or change, except closures which were applied for or planned less than one week prior to any such closure or change, which shall be available on such interactive map within seventy-two hours of the permit and approval of such closure. Where a permitted and approved street closure is due to a special event, the sponsor of the event with appropriate contact information shall be provided as part of such interactive map. For the purposes of this subdivision, special event shall mean any street fair, block party or festival on a public street(s) where such activity may interfere with or obstruct the normal use by vehicular traffic of such street(s);" (Section 1072.q)

    **Why:** To collect, maintain, and publish required information on all permitted and approved street closures for public access.

    **One row represents:** A single permitted or approved street closure event.

    **Covers:** All street closures for special events, construction, crane operations, film shoots, and paving operations.

    **Columns:**
    *   Closure ID
    *   Street Name
    *   Cross Streets/Segment Description
    *   Borough
    *   Start Date
    *   End Date
    *   Start Time
    *   End Time
    *   Closure Type (Special Event, Crane, Construction, Film Shoot, Paving)
    *   Permit Status (Permitted, Approved)
    *   Vehicular Traffic Allowed (Yes/No)
    *   "Subject to Closure" Notation (Yes/No)
    *   Special Event Sponsor Name
    *   Special Event Sponsor Contact Info
    *   Permit Application Date
    *   Permit Approval Date
    *   Last Update Date (for map)

9.  **Crime Incidents (Received from NYPD)**

    **Quotes:**
    *   "to provide to the public, at no charge on the city's website, an interactive crime map that, for each segment of a street bounded by one or more intersections and/or a terminus, shall visually display the aggregate monthly, yearly and year-to-date totals for the current and the most recent prior calendar years for each class of crime that is reported to the New York city police department, or for which an arrest was made, including crimes that occurred in parks and subway stations. Such map shall be searchable by address, zip code, and patrol precinct. All information required by this subdivision shall be available on the city's website as soon as practicable but in no case more than one month after a crime complaint has been filed. The mayor shall ensure that all agencies provide the department with such assistance and information as the department requires to compile and update the interactive crime map." (Section 1072.r)

    **Why:** To receive, process, and aggregate incident-level crime data from NYPD for the interactive crime map.

    **One row represents:** A single crime incident reported to NYPD or resulting in an arrest.

    **Columns:**
