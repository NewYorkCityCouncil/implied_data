**Part 1:**

The Fire Department's mission is to protect life, property, and public safety through fire suppression, prevention, investigation, and emergency medical services across New York City, impacting residents, businesses, and visitors. Key programs involve fire code enforcement, hazardous material regulation, and emergency response.

1.  Building Inspections and Violations (Includes fire hazards, fire alarm systems, and violations of fire laws)
2.  Certificate of Fitness Applications and Credentials
3.  Permitting (Includes hazardous materials, combustibles, hot work, and other regulated activities)
4.  Fire Alarm System Design and Installation (Includes Professional Certification, Letters of Acceptance, and Project Authorizations)
5.  Contraband Materials Management (Includes Seizures, Disposal, and Reclamation)
6.  Fuel Management (includes Storage, Dispensing, Transfers, and Leaks)
7.  Emergency Action Plan (EAP) Staff Information and Training Records
8.  Training Compliance (Includes training for elder abuse and fire safety)
9.  Inspections and Maintenance (Includes fire protection equipment, alarms, means of egress, and general maintenance)
10. Contained Fire Laws (FDNY summons, Certification of Correction, Stipulations, Violation)
