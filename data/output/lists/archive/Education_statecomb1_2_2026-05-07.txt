**Section 1, Mission Summary:**
The agency's mission is to oversee and regulate various aspects of education, from commission governance and financial management to professional licensing, special education for vulnerable populations, historical records preservation, and standardized testing, as well as providing aid and setting standards for school districts regarding instructional materials and technology. Key programs include managing the Educational Commission of the States, administering professional licenses, ensuring educational standards for children in residential care and on Indian reservations, supporting historical records programs, managing employee benefits, facilitating interstate library districts, regulating standardized testing, and providing aid for textbooks, library materials, software, and hardware to school districts, alongside ensuring accessible instructional materials for higher education students with disabilities. The agency impacts constituents by ensuring educational quality, accessibility, and accountability across various levels of the state's education system.
