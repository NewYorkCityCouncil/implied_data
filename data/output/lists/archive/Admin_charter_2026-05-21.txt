**Data Systems Plan for the Department of Citywide Administrative Services (DCAS)**

As an operations expert, I have meticulously analyzed the provided charter to identify the critical tabular datasets required for the Department of Citywide Administrative Services (DCAS) to fulfill its legal obligations and effectively manage its diverse operations. The datasets are structured by workflow and department, ensuring accuracy, security, and utility for management decision-making and oversight.
