**Identified Tabular Datasets**

**1. Funding Disbursements**

**Quotes:**
 - "to disburse available city, state and federal funds to programs throughout the city and, when practical, coordinate such funds with available funding from the private sector" (Section 2402.e)

**Why:** To fulfill its core duty of disbursing funds, the agency must meticulously track each financial transaction.

**One row represents:** A single disbursement of funds.

**Covers:** All city, state, federal, and coordinated private funds disbursed to programs and facilities.

**Columns:**
 - Disbursement ID
 - Date of Disbursement
 - Amount Disbursed
 - Funding Source (e.g., City, State, Federal, Private)
 - Program/Facility ID (links to Dataset 2)
 - Recipient Organization Name
 - Grant/Contract ID (links to Dataset 3)
 - Purpose/Description of Funds

**2. Program and Facility Inventory**

**Quotes:**
 - "to promulgate rules and regulations for the operation of facilities, services and programs under its jurisdiction" (Section 2402.g)
 - "to maintain, operate and control such programs and facilities as may be necessary or required for the proper administration of the department." (Section 2402.h)

**Why:** To manage and oversee programs and facilities, the agency needs a comprehensive record of each one.

**One row represents:** A single program or facility under the department's jurisdiction or operation.

**Covers:** All programs, services, and facilities that the department has jurisdiction over, operates, or funds.

**Columns:**
 - Program/Facility ID
 - Name of Program/Facility
 - Type (e.g., Senior Center, Workforce Development Program, Outreach Initiative)
 - Location/Address
 - Jurisdiction Status (e.g., Operated by Dept, Funded by Dept, Regulated by Dept)
 - Operational Status (e.g., Active, Inactive, Under Development)
 - Date Established/Opened
 - Contact Information
 - Capacity (if applicable)

**3. Public Housing Senior Center Complaint Log**

**Quotes:**
 - "Establishing a system to receive and process complaints regarding senior center facilities located on property owned by the New York city housing authority, including assisting senior center participants with concerns about repairs at such facilities" (Section 2404.a)

**Why:** To fulfill its mandated duty, the agency must systematically track and manage all complaints received.

**One row represents:** A single complaint regarding a senior center facility on NYCHA property.

**Covers:** All complaints received and processed by the public housing liaison regarding senior centers located on NYCHA property.

**Columns:**
 - Complaint ID
 - Date Received
 - Senior Center ID (links to Dataset 2)
 - NYCHA Property ID
 - Complainant Contact Information (if provided)
 - Complaint Type (e.g., Repair, Facility Condition, Staff Conduct, Service Quality)
 - Detailed Description of Complaint
 - Date Assigned to Liaison
 - Assigned Liaison Employee ID (links to Dataset 10)
 - Resolution Status (e.g., Open, In Progress, Resolved, Closed)
 - Date Resolved
 - Resolution Details/Notes

**4. Contract and Grant Management**

**Quotes:**
 - "to disburse available city, state and federal funds to programs throughout the city and, when practical, coordinate such funds with available funding from the private sector" (Section 2402.e)

**Why:** Disbursing funds and coordinating with external entities necessitates tracking formal agreements and their terms.

**One row represents:** A single contract or grant agreement.

**Covers:** All contracts and grants entered into by the department for funding programs, services, or operations.

**Columns:**
 - Contract/Grant ID
 - Program/Facility ID (links to Dataset 2)

 - Contract/Grant Type (e.g., Service Agreement, Grant Award)
 - Start Date
 - End Date
 - Total Award Amount
 - Current Status (e.g., Active, Expired, Completed, Terminated)
 - Key Deliverables/Milestones
 - Departmental Contact Employee ID (links to Dataset 10)

**5. Public Referral Log**

**Quotes:**
 - "to refer the public to appropriate departments and agencies of the city, state and federal governments for advice, assistance and available services in connection with particular problems" (Section 2402.b)

**Why:** To ensure effective service delivery, the agency must track all referrals made to other government entities.

**One row represents:** A single referral made by the department.

**Covers:** All instances where the department refers members of the public to other city, state, or federal agencies.

**Columns:**
 - Referral ID
 - Date of Referral
 - Referrer Staff Employee ID (links to Dataset 10)

 - Referral Method (e.g., Phone, Email, Direct Hand-off)

 - Notes

**6. Advisory Council Member Roster**

**Quotes:**
 - "There shall be in the department an advisory council consisting of thirty-one members at least sixteen of whom shall be recipients of services rendered to older adults." (Section 2403.a)
 - "The members of the council shall be appointed by the mayor." (Section 2403.c)
 - "The terms of office of the thirty-one members of the council first appointed shall be as follows: ... Upon the expiration of such terms, the terms of office of their successors shall be three years. Vacancies occurring otherwise than by expiration of term shall be filled for the unexpired term in the same manner as regular appointments." (Section 2403.d)
 - "The mayor shall designate one of the members of the council to be chairman and one to be vice-chairman." (Section 2403.e)

**Why:** To ensure proper composition and terms, the agency must maintain a detailed record of all advisory council members.

**One row represents:** A single advisory council member.

**Covers:** All individuals appointed to the Department for the Aging Advisory Council.

**Columns:**
 - Member ID
 - Name
 - Appointment Date
 - Term Start Date
 - Term End Date
 - Current Role (e.g., Member, Chairman, Vice-Chairman)
 - Representation Area (e.g., Social Service, Health Care, Business, Legal Services, Academic, Local Neighborhood)
 - Borough of Residence
 - Recipient of Services (Yes/No)
 - Appointing Authority (e.g., Mayor, City Council Recommendation)
 - Contact Information
