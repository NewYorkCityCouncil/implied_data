**Identified Tabular Datasets**

**1. Fund Disbursement and Grant Tracking**

**Quotes:**
 - "to disburse available city, state and federal funds to programs throughout the city and, when practical, coordinate such funds with available funding from the private sector;" (Section 2402.e)

**Why:** To fulfill its core duty of funding programs, the agency must track all financial disbursements and coordination efforts.

**One row represents:** A single fund disbursement, grant award, or funding coordination event.

**Covers:** All city, state, federal, and coordinated private sector funds disbursed to programs.

**Columns:**
 - Disbursement ID
 - Program ID (links to Dataset 2)
 - Recipient Organization Name
 - Funding Source (e.g., City, State, Federal, Private)
 - Amount Disbursed
 - Disbursement Date
 - Grant Start Date
 - Grant End Date
 - Private Sector Coordination (Yes/No)
 - Private Sector Funding Amount (if applicable)
 - Grant Status (e.g., Awarded, Disbursed, Closed)

**2. Program Inventory**

**Quotes:**
 - "to disburse available city, state and federal funds to programs throughout the city" (Section 2402.e)
 - "to maintain, operate and control such programs and facilities as may be necessary or required for the proper administration of the department." (Section 2402.h)

**Why:** The agency must track all programs it operates or funds to ensure proper administration and oversight.

**One row represents:** A single program under the department's jurisdiction or receiving funding.

**Covers:** All programs directly operated by the department and those receiving city, state, or federal funds.

**Columns:**
 - Program ID
 - Program Name
 - Program Description
 - Program Type (e.g., Direct Service, Grant-Funded, Community Development)
 - Department Operated (Yes/No)
 - Start Date
 - End Date (if applicable)
 - Status (e.g., Active, Inactive, Planning)
 - Target Population
 - Associated Facility ID (links to Dataset 3, if applicable)
 - Lead Department/Division

**3. Facility Inventory**

**Quotes:**
 - "to maintain, operate and control such programs and facilities as may be necessary or required for the proper administration of the department." (Section 2402.h)
 - "Establishing a system to receive and process complaints regarding senior center facilities located on property owned by the New York city housing authority" (Section 2404.a)

**Why:** To properly administer and oversee its operations, the agency needs a comprehensive record of all facilities.

**One row represents:** A single facility under the department's jurisdiction or used by its programs.

**Covers:** All facilities maintained, operated, or controlled by the department, including senior centers on NYCHA property.

**Columns:**
 - Facility ID
 - Facility Name
 - Address
 - Facility Type (e.g., Senior Center, Administrative Office, Community Space)
 - Ownership Type (e.g., City-owned, NYCHA-owned, Leased)
 - Operational Status (e.g., Open, Closed, Under Renovation)
 - Capacity
 - Associated Program IDs (links to Dataset 2)
 - Public Housing (NYCHA) Property (Yes/No)

**4. Public Housing Senior Center Complaint Log**

**Quotes:**
 - "Establishing a system to receive and process complaints regarding senior center facilities located on property owned by the New York city housing authority, including assisting senior center participants with concerns about repairs at such facilities;" (Section 2404.a)

**Why:** A specific legal requirement mandates a system to track and process complaints for a defined subset of facilities.

**One row represents:** A single complaint received by the public housing liaison.

**Covers:** Complaints regarding senior center facilities located on New York City Housing Authority (NYCHA) property.

**Columns:**
 - Complaint ID
 - Date Received
 - Complainant Name (or anonymized ID)
 - Complainant Contact Information
 - Facility ID (links to Dataset 3)
 - Description of Complaint
 - Complaint Type (e.g., Repair, Staff Conduct, Program Issue)
 - Date Assigned to Liaison
 - Liaison ID (links to Dataset 15)
 - Status (e.g., Open, Investigating, Resolved, Escalated)
 - Resolution Details
 - Date Resolved
 - Follow-up Actions

**6. Advisory Council Member Roster**

**Quotes:**
 - "There shall be in the department an advisory council consisting of thirty-one members at least sixteen of whom shall be recipients of services rendered to older adults. Beginning in December 2021, these members shall include representatives from the areas of social service, health care, business, legal services, the academic community and local neighborhoods." (Section 2403.a)
 - "The members of the council shall be appointed by the mayor. Ten of said members, two residents from each of the five boroughs of the city, shall be recommended for appointment by a majority vote of the city council members of the respective boroughs." (Section 2403.c)
 - "The terms of office of the thirty-one members of the council first appointed shall be as follows: ... Upon the expiration of such terms, the terms of office of their successors shall be three years. Vacancies occurring otherwise than by expiration of term shall be filled for the unexpired term in the same manner as regular appointments." (Section 2403.d)
 - "The mayor shall designate one of the members of the council to be chairman and one to be vice-chairman." (Section 2403.e)

**Why:** To ensure compliance with composition requirements and manage council membership, the agency must track member details.

**One row represents:** A single member's appointment to the Advisory Council.

**Covers:** All current and past members of the Advisory Council.

**Columns:**
 - Member ID
 - Member Name
 - Appointment Date
 - Term Start Date
 - Term End Date
 - Appointing Authority (e.g., Mayor, City Council)
 - Borough Represented (if applicable)
 - Representation Area (e.g., Social Service, Health Care, Business)
 - Recipient of Services (Yes/No)
 - Role (e.g., Chair, Vice-Chair, Member)
 - Status (e.g., Active, Term Expired, Vacancy)
