*   **Comprehensive City Traffic Plan:** The document itself (§2903.a.4).

*   **Proposals for Amendment of Resolutions, Rules, or Regulations:** Legislative and policy documents (§2903.a.8).
*   **Analyses of Traffic Accidents:** The detailed analytical reports, distinct from the raw accident data (§2903.a.11).
*   **Educational Activity Materials:** Brochures, videos, and other content used for public education (§2903.a.12).
*   **Special Vehicle Identification Cards:** The physical cards issued to disabled persons (§2903.a.13).
*   **Disability Certification Standards and Guidelines:** Documents outlining criteria for disability certification (§2903.a.15.a).
*   **Claim Forms for Comptroller:** Blank forms provided to property owners for filing claims (§2903.a.18).
*   **Signs Advising Public of Rights:** Physical signs posted in borough offices (§2903.a.18).
*   **Regulations for Seasonal Horticultural Operations:** Policy documents defining exceptions for encroachment removal (§2903.b.7).
*   **Bridge and Tunnel Condition Reports:** The annual reports submitted to the mayor, city council, and public (§2903.b.6).
*   **Program for Maintenance of Bridges and Tunnels:** The strategic document outlining maintenance plans (§2903.b.6).
*   **Lease Agreements for Wharf Property:** Legal documents for property leases (§2903.c.2).
*   **Publicity and Advertising Materials for Ferry Tours:** Marketing content (§2903.c.4).
*   **Plans and Recommendations for Mass Transportation Facilities:** Strategic documents and proposals (§2903.d.1).
*   **Standards and Policies of the Transportation Department (for property owner obligations):** Policy documents (§2904).
*   **Survey, Boring, or Investigation Documents:** Detailed reports and findings from property entries (§2905).
*   **Warning Signs for Construction Sites:** The physical signs posted (§2906).
