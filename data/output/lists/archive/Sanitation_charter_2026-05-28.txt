*   **Testimony Records:** Written or recorded statements given as testimony (Section 753.e.1).
*   **Evidence Documents:** Physical or digital evidence collected during investigations (Section 753.e.1).
*   **Subpoena Documents:** Copies of subpoenas issued (Section 753.e.1).
*   **Plans, Designs, and Construction Documents for Facilities:** Architectural drawings, engineering specifications, and project plans for incinerators, landfills, and other plants (Section 753.a.5).
*   **Commercial Waste Removal Contracts:** The full legal agreements between commercial waste collectors and their clients (Section 753.e.2.c).
*   **Commercial Waste Billing Forms:** Templates or examples of billing forms used by commercial waste collectors (Section 753.e.2.d).
*   **Commercial Waste Business Records:** Records maintained by commercial waste businesses that the department inspects (e.g., internal logs, financial statements) (Section 753.e.2.e).
*   **Commercial Waste Insurance Policies:** The actual insurance policy documents held by commercial waste collectors (Section 753.e.2.f).
*   **Public and Commercial Education Program Materials:** Brochures, presentations, videos, and other content used for educational outreach (Section 753.e.3).
