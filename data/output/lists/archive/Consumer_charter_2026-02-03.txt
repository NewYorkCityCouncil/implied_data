**Identified Tabular Datasets**

**1. Notices of Violation (NOVs)**

**Quotes:**
- "Each inspector or other employee of the department who issues notices of violation shall, for provisions of law that are enforced exclusively by the department, have access at the time that a violation is issued to equipment allowing such person: (1) to determine if such violation is a first-time violation of the applicable provision of law, and (2) if permitted by law, to issue a warning for such violation and to record that such a warning has been issued." (Section 2203.f)
- "All such proceedings shall be commenced by the service of a notice of violation." (Section 2203.h.2)
- "The original or a copy of the notice of violation shall be filed and retained by the department and shall be deemed a record kept in the ordinary course of business." (Section 2203.h.2)
- "Proof of service made pursuant to paragraph seven of this subdivision shall be maintained by, and preserved within, the department for at least six years following the date of the final decision or order relating to the adjudication of the subject notice of violation." (Section 2203.h.9)

**Why:** Legally required to track all NOVs, their service, and violation details for enforcement and record-keeping.

**One row represents:** A single Notice of Violation issued by the department.

**Covers:** All notices of violation issued by the department across all enforcement areas (consumer protection, worker protection, weights and measures, advertising, labor standards).

**Columns:**
- NOV ID
- **Related Investigation ID (Dataset 2)**
- Date Issued
- Issuing Inspector ID
- Respondent Type (e.g., licensee, non-licensee, business, individual)
- Respondent Name
- Respondent Business Name (if applicable)
- Respondent Address
- Violation Type (e.g., weights & measures, advertising, labor law, license)
- Specific Law/Rule Violated
- Violation Description
- Is First-Time Violation? (Y/N)
- Warning Issued (Y/N)
- Date Warning Issued
- Service Method (e.g., mail, personal, affixing, electronic)
- Date Served
- Proof of Service Details (e.g., certified mail tracking, affidavit)
- **Related Adjudication Case ID (Dataset 3)**

**3. Adjudication Cases and Decisions**

**Quotes:**
- "The department shall have the power to render decisions and orders." (Section 2203.h.1)
- "The department shall be authorized, upon due notice and hearing, to impose civil penalties for, and to order restitution or other forms of equitable relief, and payment of monetary damages, to a consumer or worker" (Section 2203.h.1)
- "Where the department has delegated any adjudicatory powers to the office of administrative trials and hearings, for all cases heard by the adjudicatory body authorized to conduct trials in such office, the office of administrative trials and hearings shall issue a recommended decision which the commissioner may adopt, reverse, modify, or remand" (Section 2203.h.1)
- "failure to plead or appear shall be deemed, for all purposes, to be an admission of liability for the purposes of such violation and shall be grounds for rendering a default decision and order imposing a penalty" (Section 2203.h.3)

**Why:** To track the legal process, outcomes, and financial determinations of all violations.

**One row represents:** A single adjudication case resulting from a Notice of Violation.

**Covers:** All adjudication cases heard by the department or OATH, including default decisions.

**Columns:**
- Adjudication Case ID
- **Related NOV ID (Dataset 1)**
- Respondent Name
- Respondent Business Name (if applicable)
- Hearing Date
- Hearing Officer/OATH ID
- Plea (e.g., Admit, Deny, Default)
- Decision Date
- Decision Outcome (e.g., Liable, Not Liable, Default, Dismissed)
- Civil Penalty Imposed Amount
- Restitution Ordered Amount
- Monetary Damages Ordered Amount
- Equitable Relief Ordered Description
- Total Amount Due (Penalties + Restitution + Damages)
- Payment Status (e.g., Paid, Outstanding, Partial)
- **Related Judgment ID (Dataset 4)**

**4. Judgments and Enforcement Actions**

**Quotes:**
- "Any final decision or order of the department relating to a designated violation, whether the adjudication was had by hearing or upon default or otherwise, shall constitute a judgment that may be entered and docketed in the civil court of the city of New York or any other place provided for the entry and docketing of civil judgments within the state and may be enforced without court proceedings in the same manner as the enforcement of money judgments entered and docketed in civil actions." (Section 2203.h.5)
- "before a judgment based upon a default may be so entered and docketed, the department must have notified the respondent in accordance with this paragraph." (Section 2203.h.6)
- "At the request of a consumer or a worker, the department shall assign, without consideration or liability, a final decision or order, provided that such decision or order solely authorizes restitution, imposes an award of monetary damages or provides equitable relief to such consumer or a worker, or that portion of such award that authorizes restitution, imposes an award of monetary damages or provides equitable relief to such consumer or a worker. After such assignment, the department shall not be required to take any further action to enforce such restitution, award of monetary damages or equitable relief to such consumer." (Section 2203.h.4)

**Why:** To track the legal enforceability, collection, and assignment of penalties and relief.

**One row represents:** A single judgment entered or a decision/order assigned.

**Covers:** All final decisions or orders that are converted into judgments or assigned to consumers/workers.

**Columns:**
- Judgment ID
- **Related Adjudication Case ID (Dataset 3)**
- Date Judgment Entered/Assigned
- Court/Docketing Location (if applicable)
- Total Judgment Amount
- Amount Collected
- Collection Status (e.g., Collected, Outstanding, Assigned)
- Date of Default Judgment Notice (if applicable)
- Default Judgment Notice Service Details (if applicable)
- Is Assigned (Y/N)
- Date Assigned (if assigned)
- Assignee Details (if assigned)
