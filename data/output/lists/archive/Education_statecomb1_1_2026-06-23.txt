**Section 1, Mission Summary:**

The agency's mission is to oversee and regulate education and child labor within the state, ensuring the safety, well-being, and educational rights of children and the integrity of educational programs. Key programs include regulating child employment, monitoring school attendance and census, ensuring educational access for children in foster care and residential facilities, enforcing data privacy and anti-bullying policies in schools, and managing various grants and professional licenses. The agency impacts constituents by safeguarding children's rights, promoting educational equity, and maintaining standards for educational institutions and professionals.
