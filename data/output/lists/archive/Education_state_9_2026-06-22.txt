**Non-Tabular Information:**

*   **Regulations of the Commissioner:** (e.g., for educational costs, religious observance, parent accompaniment for transportation, student discipline procedures). These are policy documents.
*   **Individualized Education Programs (IEPs):** While they contain structured data, the IEP itself is a comprehensive document, not a single tabular record for this context. Specific services from an IEP (like special transportation) would be referenced in other datasets.
*   **Written Explanations for Dispute Appeals:** The content of the explanation is text, not tabular data. The *fact* of providing it is a column in Dataset 14.
*   **Plan for Coordination and Access to Education for Homeless Children:** This is a strategic document. Its annual review is a process, not a dataset.
*   **Local Regulations, Policies, or Practices (barriers to homeless child enrollment):** These are policy documents.
*   **Child Abuse Prevention Policies and Procedures:** These are policy documents.
*   **Code of Conduct (for student discipline):** This is a policy document.
*   **Legally Binding Instruments (e.g., court order or decree of divorce, separation or custody):** These are legal documents, not tabular data. Their existence might be noted in a student's record.
*   **Testimony and Evidence from Disciplinary Hearings:** These are records of proceedings, not tabular data. The *record of the hearing* is a column in Dataset 8.
*   **Description of Behavior and/or Academic Problems (for involuntary transfers):** This is narrative text within a recommendation document.
*   **Description of Alternatives Explored and Prior Action Taken (for involuntary transfers):** This is narrative text within a recommendation document.
*   **List of Community Agencies and Free Legal Assistance (for involuntary transfers):** This is a resource list.
