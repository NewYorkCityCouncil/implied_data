**Section 1, Mission Summary:**

The agency's mission is to ensure the proper education, safety, and welfare of children and minors, while upholding data privacy and security standards within the educational system. Key programs include regulating child labor through employment certificates, overseeing educational placements for children in foster care, enforcing anti-harassment and anti-discrimination policies in schools, and managing data privacy for student, teacher, and principal information. The agency impacts constituents by safeguarding children's rights to education and safe employment, and protecting their sensitive data.
