**Section 1, Mission Summary:**

The Fire Department is responsible for the government, discipline, management, maintenance, and direction of the fire department, including extinguishing fires, providing emergency medical services, enforcing fire safety laws, and investigating fires within the city's jurisdiction. The agency protects lives and property by responding to emergencies, preventing fires, and ensuring compliance with fire safety regulations.
