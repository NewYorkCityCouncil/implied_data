**36. Committee Recommendation Submissions (AANHPI History)**

**Quotes:**
*   "The committee shall provide recommendations on the following: (a) high-quality K-12 curricular and instructional resources on AANHPI history; and (b) strategies for strengthening and deepening public K-12 education programs in the areas of AANHPI history, including analysis of the survey referenced in section two of the chapter of the laws of two thousand twenty-five that added this section." (§ 805.5)
*   "The committee shall submit their recommendations to the commissioner no later than two years after the effective date of this section and be disbanded thereafter." (§ 805.6)

**Why:** To track the submission of recommendations by the AANHPI History Advisory Committee to the commissioner.

**One row represents:** A single submission of recommendations by the AANHPI History Advisory Committee.

**Covers:** Recommendations provided by the AANHPI History Advisory Committee.

**Columns:**
*   Submission ID
*   Submission Date
*   Commissioner Receipt Date
*   Recommendation Type (e.g., Curricular Resources, Program Strategies)
*   Summary of Recommendations
*   Status (e.g., Received, Under Review, Implemented)
*   **Linked to Dataset 27 (Advisory Committee Meeting Records (AANHPI History))** (if recommendations were finalized at a meeting)
