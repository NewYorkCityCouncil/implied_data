**Section 1, Mission Summary:**

The agency's mission is to ensure all minors receive appropriate instruction, manage educational services, and uphold compliance with state and federal education laws. They are required to run programs for student enrollment, attendance, special education, limited English proficiency, homeless and incarcerated youth, and non-public school oversight. The agency impacts constituents by guaranteeing access to education, providing necessary support services, and ensuring a safe and compliant learning environment for all students.
