**1. Social Services Case Records**

**Quotes:**
 - Section 603: "the commissioner shall have the powers and perform the duties of a commissioner of social services under the social services law, provided that no form of outdoor relief shall be dispensed by the city except under the provisions of a state or local law which shall specifically provide the method, manner and conditions of dispensing the same."

**Why:** To fulfill its core mission, the agency must track individuals receiving social services and their eligibility.

**One row represents:** A single social services case for an individual or household.

**Covers:** All individuals or households receiving social services from the department, including those receiving outdoor relief.

**Columns:**
 - Case ID
 - Client ID
 - Client Name
 - Client Address
 - Household Size
 - Case Open Date
 - Case Status (Active, Closed, Pending)
 - Eligibility Determination Date
 - Eligibility Criteria Met
 - Primary Social Worker ID (linked to Dataset 6)
 - Approved Service Types (e.g., financial assistance, housing support, food aid)
 - Referral Source
 - Case Close Date (if applicable)
 - Reason for Closure

**2. Outdoor Relief Disbursement Records**

**Quotes:**
 - Section 603: "no form of outdoor relief shall be dispensed by the city except under the provisions of a state or local law which shall specifically provide the method, manner and conditions of dispensing the same."

**Why:** The agency must track the specific transactions of outdoor relief to ensure compliance with legal provisions.

**One row represents:** A single instance of outdoor relief dispensed to a client.

**Covers:** All disbursements of outdoor relief provided by the agency.

**Columns:**
 - Disbursement ID
 - Case ID (linked to Dataset 1)
 - Client ID (linked to Dataset 1)
 - Disbursement Date
 - Disbursement Type (e.g., direct financial aid, voucher, in-kind)
 - Amount/Value of Disbursement
 - Method of Disbursement (e.g., direct deposit, check, physical voucher)
 - Conditions Met for Disbursement (e.g., specific need, emergency status)
 - Legal Provision/Law Authorizing Disbursement
 - Approving Staff ID (linked to Dataset 6)
 - Status (Approved, Denied, Disbursed)

**3. Agency Institutions Inventory**

**Quotes:**
 - Section 604: "The commissioner shall control, maintain and operate such institutions as are now or may be put under his or her control."

**Why:** To control, maintain, and operate institutions, the agency needs a comprehensive list of all its facilities.

**One row represents:** A single public institution under the commissioner's control.

**Covers:** All public institutions controlled, maintained, and operated by the Department of Social Services.

**Columns:**
 - Institution ID
 - Institution Name
 - Institution Address
 - Institution Type (e.g., shelter, community center, administrative office)
 - Capacity (e.g., number of beds, occupancy limit)
 - Date Established/Acquired
 - Current Status (Operational, Under Renovation, Closed)
 - Managing Department/Division
 - Primary Contact Person ID (linked to Dataset 6)

**4. Institution Maintenance Activity Records**

**Quotes:**
 - Section 604: "The commissioner shall control, maintain and operate such institutions as are now or may be put under his or her control."

**Why:** To maintain institutions, the agency must track all maintenance requests, activities, and resolutions.

**One row represents:** A single maintenance request or activity performed at an institution.

**Covers:** All maintenance activities, both scheduled and unscheduled, for agency-controlled institutions.

**Columns:**
 - Maintenance Activity ID
 - Institution ID (linked to Dataset 3)
 - Request Date
 - Reported By (Staff ID, linked to Dataset 6)
 - Problem Description
 - Maintenance Type (e.g., plumbing, electrical, structural, HVAC)
 - Priority Level
 - Scheduled Date
 - Completion Date
 - Performed By (Internal Staff ID or Vendor Name)
 - Cost of Repair
 - Resolution Description
 - Status (Open, In Progress, Completed, Canceled)

**5. Institution Operational Activity Records**

**Quotes:**
 - Section 604: "The commissioner shall control, maintain and operate such institutions as are now or may be put under his or her control."

**Why:** To operate institutions effectively, the agency needs to track daily activities, incidents, and resource utilization.

**One row represents:** A specific operational event, incident, or daily metric at an institution.

**Covers:** Key operational events, incidents, and daily metrics across all agency-controlled institutions.

**Columns:**
 - Operational Record ID
 - Institution ID (linked to Dataset 3)
 - Date of Record/Event
 - Event Type (e.g., daily census, incident report, staffing change, program delivery)
 - Description of Event/Metric
 - Relevant Value (e.g., number of residents, incident severity, staff count)
 - Reporting Staff ID (linked to Dataset 6)
 - Follow-up Actions Required
 - Status (Open, Closed)

**6. Agency Employee Roster**

**Quotes:**
 - Section 601: "There shall be a department of social services the head of which shall be the commissioner of social services."
 - Section 602: "The commissioner may appoint three deputies."

**Why:** To manage its staff and organizational structure, the agency needs to track all employees and their roles.

**One row represents:** A single employee within the Department of Social Services.

**Covers:** All employees, including the commissioner and deputies, within the Department of Social Services.

**Columns:**
 - Employee ID
 - First Name
 - Last Name
 - Job Title
 - Department/Division
 - Supervisor ID (linked to Employee ID within this dataset)
 - Appointment Date
 - Employment Status (Active, Leave, Terminated)
 - Contact Information (Email, Phone)
 - Required Certifications/Licenses
 - Date of Last Certification Renewal
