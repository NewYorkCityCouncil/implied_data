**Section 1, Mission Summary:**

The agency's mission is to ensure the proper education, safety, and welfare of children and minors, while upholding data privacy and security standards within the educational system, and overseeing various aspects of education from commission governance and financial management to professional licensing, special education for vulnerable populations, historical records preservation, and standardized testing. Key programs include regulating child labor, overseeing educational placements for children in foster care and residential care, enforcing anti-harassment and anti-discrimination policies, managing data privacy for student, teacher, and principal information, administering professional licenses, supporting historical records programs, managing employee benefits, facilitating interstate library districts, regulating standardized testing, and providing aid for instructional materials to school districts. The agency impacts constituents by safeguarding children's rights to education and safe employment, protecting their sensitive data, ensuring educational quality, accessibility, and accountability across various levels of the state's education system.
