**Section 1, Mission Summary:**

This agency is primarily responsible for overseeing and supporting public and non-public education, vocational rehabilitation, and independent living services for individuals with disabilities across the state. Its key programs include managing school operations, student health and safety, vocational training and employment support, and financial oversight for educational and rehabilitation services. The agency impacts constituents by ensuring access to quality education, promoting student well-being, facilitating vocational opportunities, and upholding financial accountability in public programs.
