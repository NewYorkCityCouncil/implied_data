**Non-Tabular Information (Examples from the text)**

*   **Rules and Regulations:** The entire text itself is a collection of rules and regulations. The agency promulgates and amends these, but they are not tabular data. (e.g., § 16-117, § 16-131, § 16-208, § 16-323, § 16-432, § 16-477, § 16-485)
*   **Reports (Aggregated):** Many sections mention reports (e.g., annual reports, preliminary reports, studies). While these reports *contain* data, they are typically aggregated summaries or analyses, not the raw, incident-level tabular data requested. (e.g., § 16-111.2.d, § 16-118.1.d, § 16-134.c, § 16-204, § 16-305.h, § 16-306.c, § 16-307.c, § 16-307.2.d, § 16-307.3.d, § 16-308.h, § 16-308.1.d, § 16-308.2.g, § 16-310.d, § 16-310.1.c, § 16-310.3.f, § 16-316.1, § 16-316.3.b/c, § 16-316.4.b/c, § 16-316.5, § 16-428, § 16-473, § 16-498.5)
*   **Plans/Strategies:** Documents outlining how the agency will achieve goals or implement programs. (e.g., § 16-120.1.f, § 16-124.1.b, § 16-140, § 16-306.1.b, § 16-307.b, § 16-307.1.b, § 16-307.2.b, § 16-307.3.b, § 16-308.1.b, § 16-315.b, § 16-316.5.a, § 16-423.d)
*   **Memoranda of Understanding (MOU):** Agreements between agencies. (e.g., § 16-114.2.d)
*   **Legal Opinions/Determinations:** (e.g., § 16-112, § 16-116.c)
*   **Public Notices/Information:** Content of signs, website information, guides. (e.g., § 16-111.2.c, § 16-119.h, § 16-315.a, § 16-425.c)
*   **Research Documents/Studies:** The output of a study, not the raw data. (e.g., § 16-316, § 16-316.1, § 16-316.3.c)
*   **Job Applications:** While employee data is tabular, the application itself is typically a document.
*   **Receipts:** Transactional data is tabular, but the physical receipt is a document.
*   **Photos/Images:** (e.g., for signs, uniforms)
*   **Contracts:** While contract *details* might be tabular (e.g., **Commercial Waste Collection Service Agreements**), the full contract document is not. (e.g., § 16-134.b)
*   **Community Board Resolutions:** (e.g., § 16-120.c.4)
