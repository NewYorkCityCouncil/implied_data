Section 1, Mission Summary:
The Emergency Management Department's mission is to coordinate and manage the city's multi-agency response to all emergencies and potential incidents, ensuring public health and safety through comprehensive planning, preparedness activities, and rapid resource mobilization. The agency impacts constituents by protecting them during crises, providing essential relief and recovery services, and disseminating critical information to help them prepare and respond effectively.

**1. Emergency Incident Log**
*   **Quotes:**
    *   "coordinate the city's response to all emergency conditions and potential incidents which require a multi-agency response, including but not limited to severe weather, threats from natural hazards and natural disasters, power and other public service outages, labor unrest other than the keeping of the peace, water main breaks, transportation and transit incidents, hazardous substance discharges, building collapses, aviation disasters, explosions, acts of terrorism and such other emergency conditions and incidents which affect public health and safety" (§ 497.a)
    *   "monitor on a constant basis all potential emergency conditions and potential incidents which may require a multi-agency response" (§ 497.b)

    *   Status (Active, Resolved, Standby, Monitoring)

    *   Associated Emergency Plan Activated (linked to Dataset 2)
    *   Summary of Actions Taken
    *   Fatality Count
    *   Injury Count
    *   Estimated Economic Damage
    *   Funding Applied For (linked to Dataset 18)
