**Section 1, Mission Summary:**

This agency is responsible for overseeing and regulating various aspects of education, from standardized testing to K-12 school operations and higher education instructional materials. Its key programs include administering and reporting on standardized tests, ensuring fire safety and emergency preparedness in schools and colleges, mandating and auditing curriculum compliance, managing state aid for instructional technology, and regulating the adoption and accessibility of instructional materials in both K-12 and higher education. The agency's impacts on constituents include ensuring fair and transparent testing, promoting student safety, guaranteeing equitable access to educational resources, and upholding educational standards across the state.
