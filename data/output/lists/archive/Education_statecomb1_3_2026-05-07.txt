The agency's mission is to ensure the safety, health, and educational quality of students across K-12 public, private, and college institutions by enforcing state mandates, providing resources, and overseeing various programs. They are required to run programs related to fire safety, emergency preparedness, curriculum compliance, instructional technology aid, teacher certification and training, student health services, grant administration, and advisory committee operations. The agency impacts constituents by safeguarding student well-being, ensuring educational standards, and facilitating access to resources and specialized instruction.
