**Section 1, Mission Summary:**

This agency is responsible for overseeing and implementing educational and vocational rehabilitation programs, with a strong emphasis on student health, safety, and academic achievement, as well as supporting individuals with disabilities in achieving vocational independence. The agency is required to run key programs including student health services (immunizations, screenings, medication administration), school meal programs, special education support, vocational rehabilitation services, and various educational compliance and reporting initiatives. The agency's impacts on constituents include ensuring student well-being and readiness for learning, promoting academic success, facilitating access to career opportunities for individuals with disabilities, and maintaining safe and compliant school environments.

*   **Application and Consent Forms:** Signed job applications, physician orders, parental consent forms for medication administration, and written substantiation of religious objections (e.g., for dissection, health exams).
*   **Policy and Program Documents:** Rules and regulations (commissioner's, board of regents, federal), school district policies (e.g., dissection policy, meal shaming plan, sudden cardiac arrest plan), curriculum documents (e.g., internet safety curricula, reading instructional best practices), written program plans (for animal experimentation waivers, health training), program plans for health training for secondary students, policies and procedures for supported employment programs.
*   **Reports and Studies:** Research documents, reports to the governor and legislature (e.g., on animal experimentation waivers, integrated employment implementation), annual reviews of reading curriculum and instructional practices, vocational rehabilitation studies, investigations, demonstrations, research, reports, state interagency plan for integrated employment opportunities, annual reports on integrated employment implementation plan, vocational education and extension board annual reports, vocational education and extension board survey reports on career education and extension needs, gifts and donations annual report.
*   **Financial and Administrative Records:** Receipts, compensation agreement details, audit protocols for verifying costs of integrated employment programs, sharing agreements for health professionals between districts.
*   **Communication and Outreach Materials:** Notices to parents (e.g., for missing health certificates, meal account balances), informational materials from public libraries, website content (e.g., advanced course notifications), newsletters used by nutrition advisory committees to inform parents, written informational material on epinephrine device use (provided to teachers), information on sudden cardiac arrest (developed by Commissioner of Health and Department, posted on websites, included in athletic permission forms), food service ingredient lists and nutritional information (detailed content provided by food service companies), water safety information documents (produced by Commissioner of Health, provided to school districts and parents), bedbug prevention information (developed by the board of education in consultation with city agencies, provided to parents).
*   **Physical Assets/Records:** Photos, insignia for State Seal of Biliteracy, signs/notices for AED locations, student cumulative health records, pupil's permanent health record.
*   **Meeting Records:** Meeting minutes and recommendations from Nutrition Advisory Committees, vocational education and extension board advisory committee records.
*   **Operational Guidelines:** Course of exercises and instruction for Conservation Day (prescribed by commissioner), lists of dental practices for free/reduced cost services, vocational education and extension board rules and regulations for transaction of business and administration of work.
