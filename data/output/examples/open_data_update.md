Subject: Parks Department - October Open Data Update

Here's the October update on datasets that the Parks Department is legally mandated to maintain by the NYC Charter and Administrative Code, but does not currently publish on NYC Open Data. If any of these missing datasets overlap with your policy focus this month and you would like us to formally request them from OTI, let us know and we can start the process.


**Trees & Urban Canopy**
* **Tree Removal Permits & Replacement Obligations**: Tracking for tree removal approvals, fee collections, and mandatory replanting.
* **Public Complaint & Service Request Log**: Logs of 311 and direct public reports for fallen branches, hazardous trees, and sidewalk damage.

**Park Operations**
* **Violation & Enforcement Records**: Citations and fines issued by Park Enforcement Patrol for rule violations, vandalism, and concession boundary overreach.
* **Marina Operations & Waiting Lists**: Permit tracking, mooring waitlists, and vessel inventories for city-operated marinas and boat basins.
* **Ballfield & Athletic Court Permits**: Allocations, reservations, and youth league AED compliance certifications across city fields.

**Park Safety**
* **Lifeguard Incident Records & Wildfire Logs**: Safety incident tracking, water rescues, and brush fire occurrences across parkland.
* **Soil Lead Testing Records**: Environmental safety assessments and soil lead remediation data for playgrounds and capital construction sites.

**Finance & Concessions**
* **Contracts, Leases & Agreements Register**: Active contracts with private vendors, venue leases, and commercial operating agreements.
* **Donation & Sponsorship Register**: Non-governmental funding, corporate sponsorships, and Adopt-a-Park grants over $5,000.
