**Section 1, Mission Summary:**

The agency's mission is to advocate for and serve older adults in the city, ensuring their well-being, independence, and access to essential services. Key programs include funding and overseeing older adult centers and social adult day cares, providing housing support, offering digital literacy training, and coordinating inter-agency efforts for the aging population. The agency impacts constituents by enhancing their quality of life, protecting their rights, and connecting them to vital resources and community support.
